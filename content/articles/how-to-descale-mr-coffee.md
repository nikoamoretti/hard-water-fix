A CLEAN light on a Mr. Coffee is scale on the heater path, not a dirty carafe. PSTX, ABX, BVMC-DT100, and BVMC-PC14 print that reminder as calcium and limestone in the water. This is not a [Hamilton Beach](/articles/how-to-descale-hamilton-beach-coffee-maker) 30-second brew. It is not a [Cuisinart](/articles/how-to-descale-cuisinart-coffee-maker) five-beep CLEAN. It is not a [Ninja](/articles/how-to-descale-ninja-coffee-maker) DualBrew clock. Read the model on the bottom. Then follow that sheet.

## What the light is

USGS: hardness is dissolved calcium and magnesium. Heat them — USGS names the home coffee maker — and you get calcium carbonate. Vinegar charges those particles so they rinse. Hard at 121 mg/L as CaCO3; very hard above 180. [Test the tap](/articles/how-to-test-water-hardness-at-home) if you want the number. This page does not size a softener.

BVMC-DT100: Clean Indicator after **90 brew cycles**, or **two consecutive brew cycles lasting longer than 9.5 minutes**. PSTX, ABX, and PC14: **80** cycles on soft water, **40** on hard water, or when the red CLEAN light flashes. A water-only brew is the rinse *after* acid. A [Keurig](/articles/how-to-descale-keurig-without-vinegar) bottle path is a different brand.

## Pull the water-filter disk

PSTX Series (BVMC-PSTX91): **Before decalcifying, remove the water filtration disk from the machine.** ABX: the same line. DT100: **Remove optional water filtration disk.** PC14: **IMPORTANT: Remove the advanced water filtration system from the coffeemaker.** Put the disk back only after the water rinses. Empty grounds. Empty paper, or rinse the permanent filter. Empty carafe on the plate.

## CLEAN-cycle path (named sheets — do not mash)

The automatic cycle is not Hamilton Beach’s 30-second ON. Mr. Coffee prints a **slow brew of about 3 cups**, a **30-minute pause with CLEAN still on**, then the rest of the solution.

**PSTX Series (BVMC-PSTX91):** One batch of Mr. Coffee coffeemaker cleaner, or **4 cups or 20 fl. oz. of undiluted, white household vinegar**. Empty 10–12 cup basket filter. Empty decanter. **Press the CLEAN button until the CLEAN indicator light turns on.** 45–60 minutes. Slow brew ~3 cups. Pause 30 minutes. Brew the remainder. CLEAN light off, unit off. Discard. Rinse the decanter. Fill with **clean, fresh water**. Full brew. Repeat that water brew **one more time**.

**ABX Series (P.N. 190138 Rev B):** Same 4 cups / 20 fl. oz. / 592 ml vinegar, or one batch of cleaner. Same disk pull. **Press the Select/Off button twice.** Clock shows **CLn**. Same slow brew / 30-minute pause / finish. When complete it **beeps 3 times**, Clean light off, unit off. Two water brews after. Three beeps on this sheet. Do not paste a Cuisinart five-beep onto it.

**BVMC-DT100 (10-cup):** **6 cups of undiluted, white household vinegar.** Not 20 ounces. Press CLEAN. Light pulses. About 45 minutes. Same 3-cup slow brew, 30-minute pause, remainder. Then **brew 2 full 10 cup cycles with clean water.** Note: **The cleaning light will NOT turn off unless you run the CLEAN FUNCTION in its entirety (this will take 45 minutes).**

**BVMC-PC14 (14-cup):** **4 cups or 20 fl. oz. of undiluted, white household vinegar.** **Hold down the STRONG button for 3 seconds** so Clean blinks and Brew Now lights. About 45 minutes. Clock turns to **CLn** when done. Rinse with **14 cups of clean, fresh water**, then that 14-cup rinse again. This sheet also prints a hold that is *not* a descale: hold STRONG 3 seconds, then **quickly press BREW NOW** to shut the indicators off without acid. Do not use that skip on PSTX or DT100.

Do not average 6 cups with 20 ounces. Do not put Select/Off-twice on a CLEAN button. Do not put a STRONG hold on PSTX.

## Older pots: vinegar brew (named sheets — do not mash)

No CLEAN button? The brand blog and the older booklets do not print the same fill.

**Mr. Coffee blog (“How To Clean A Coffee Maker The Easy Way”):** “you want to use around half as much vinegar as you have water.” That is **not** a 50/50 mix, and it is **not** a quart of undiluted vinegar. Use the clean function if you have one. If not, press Brew Now. After that brew, sit **15–30 minutes**. Dump. Then **two water-only brews**, emptying between.

**NL12 booklet:** **Pour 1 quart of undiluted white household vinegar into Coffee Maker.** Paper filter. Empty decanter. ON. When **three cups have flowed through**, OFF. Stand **1/2 hour**. Pour the vinegar back in. ON until it finishes. Discard. Flush with a **decanter full of tap water**. Repeat that tap flush. That quart is 32 ounces undiluted.

**URX20 / URX23:** **Pour four cups of undiluted white household vinegar into the water reservoir.** BREW. Stop at **three cups**, stand **30 minutes**, finish. Two **tap water** flushes. URX30 / URX33 on the same sheet: press Clean Cycle; half the vinegar flows, 30-minute pause, then the rest.

Do not mash the quart, the four cups, the 20-ounce CLEAN fill, the 6-cup DT100 fill, and the blog’s half-as-much line. Grocery white vinegar. No Amazon URL. Booklet vs pantry acid: [vinegar vs descaling solution](/articles/vinegar-vs-descaling-solution-coffee-maker).

## Optional: Mr. Coffee coffeemaker cleaner

PSTX, ABX, and NL12 name **MR. COFFEE® Cleaner** or vinegar. Follow the packet if you buy that class. This page ships vinegar as the published fallback. Grocery white vinegar. No Amazon URL.

## Rinses, and when CLEAN will not turn off

Minimum **two water rinses** after acid. PSTX and ABX: clean, fresh water, full brew, **repeat one more time**. DT100: **2 full 10 cup cycles with clean water**. PC14: **14 cups** twice. NL12 and URX: two **tap water** flushes. Fetched booklets print clean, fresh, or tap. They do not print distilled as a rinse. Distilled is omitted here.

DT100: the light stays until the **entire** CLEAN function finishes. PSTX / ABX: the light turns off when that cycle ends. PC14: cycle ends on **CLn**; the STRONG-then-BREW-NOW hold only clears the reminder **without** descaling. Do not invent one mush reset.

## The pot is not the heater

White film on the glass is a **carafe** job: [how to remove hard water stains from a coffee carafe](/articles/how-to-remove-hard-water-stains-from-coffee-carafe). PSTX/ABX: equal parts water and vinegar in the carafe about 20 minutes, then wash. A clear pot does not descale the heater. Do not pour a carafe soak into the reservoir.

## What not to do

- Do not leave the water-filter disk in when *your* sheet says pull it.
- Do not copy Hamilton Beach’s **30-second** brew / 30-minute soak. This brand’s CLEAN pause is after a slow ~3-cup brew.
- Do not copy Cuisinart’s **five-beep** CLEAN. ABX beeps **3 times**.
- Do not copy a Ninja DualBrew clock.
- Do not skip the two water rinses. Do not brew coffee with vinegar still in the tank.
- Do not pour bathroom CLR, bleach, or abrasive powder into the reservoir. CLEAN booklets print white household vinegar or Mr. Coffee Cleaner.

## FAQ

**Is CLEAN the same on every Mr. Coffee?**
No. PSTX: CLEAN until the light is on, 45–60 minutes, 20 oz. ABX: Select/Off twice, CLn, 3 beeps. DT100: CLEAN, 45 minutes, 6 cups. PC14: hold STRONG 3 seconds, 20 ounces, 14-cup rinses.

**32 ounces or half vinegar?**
NL12 prints **1 quart undiluted**. The brand blog prints **around half as much vinegar as you have water**. CLEAN sheets print **20 fl. oz.** (PSTX, ABX, PC14) or **6 cups** (DT100). Do not mash.

**Will a water brew turn CLEAN off?**
Not on DT100. That sheet wants the full CLEAN function. PC14’s STRONG hold can clear the *indicator* without acid — that is not a descale.
