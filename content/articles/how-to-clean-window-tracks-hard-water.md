## Prevention

Prevention is a dry, lubed channel after the next splash, not a harsher acid.

- Vacuum loose grit when you see it. Do not wait until the sash hops.
- After a wash or a wet week, wipe the track dry. Standing hard water dries into fresh chalk on leftover dust.
- Stick to pantry vinegar **50/50** on a cloth or soft brush for routine haze. [CLR](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C4G2BJKX?tag=hardwaterfi04-20) only on leftover **metal** crust, two-minute clock, never vinyl / plastic / painted.
- After a clean, [WD-40 Specialist Silicone](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GV1JD4J1?tag=hardwaterfi04-20) on the **dry** track.
- Keep weep holes open. A clogged weep leaves a wet stripe that scales faster.
- If the tap or irrigation tests hard or very hard, [test the tap](/articles/how-to-test-water-hardness-at-home) and expect faster return of track chalk.
