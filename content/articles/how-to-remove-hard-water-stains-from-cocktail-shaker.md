## Hard-water signs on a cocktail shaker

Know the film before you open the vinegar.

- Sticky sweet film on the walls or strainer that soap lifts: **syrup / sugar**. Soap first. Then vinegar for leftover chalk.
- Faint white haze on the tin or cap after a hard-tap rinse dries: early mineral film. Diluted vinegar wipe or short soak, soft cloth with the grain, rinse until no smell, dry fully.
- Raised white chalk a fingernail catches on the rim, the strainer holes, or the exterior: carbonate scale. Same vinegar path inside. Optional [CLR](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C4G2BJKX?tag=hardwaterfi04-20) **exterior chrome/stainless only**, two-minute clock — not the interior as default.
- Pickle smell in the next shake: leftover vinegar in the cap seal or strainer holes. Rinse again until **no vinegar smell**. **Dry fully.**
- Cloudy mixing glass that stays cloudy after chalk is gone: etched or scratched glass. Vinegar clears mineral; it does not polish glass clear again.
- Copper or plated exterior that dulls after a harsh sit: stop. CLR stay-off. Short vinegar wipe only if the maker allows.
