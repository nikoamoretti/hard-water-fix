## Hard-water signs in a toothbrush holder

Know the film before you open the vinegar.

- Sticky gray film on the cup floor that soap lifts: toothpaste slime / biofilm. Soap first. Then vinegar for leftover chalk.
- Faint white haze on the cup floor or slots after drips dry: early mineral film. Vinegar **1:3**, soft brush, rinse, dry upside down.

- Slimy puddle that smells stale under the chalk: biofilm plus mineral. Soap first. Then the vinegar soak — do not skip the soap pass.

- Scratched plastic that re-hazes fast after every clean: surface harbors film. Dry upside down and empty drips sooner — prevention matters more than stronger acid.
