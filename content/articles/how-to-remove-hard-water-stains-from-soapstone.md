## Why acid still etches soapstone

The crust you want gone is mostly calcium carbonate. The stone is mostly talc. The magnesite is not. Magnesite is magnesium carbonate. Acid that dissolves the crust also hits that carbonate and the oil on the face. Dynamic Stone Tools: white vinegar is acetic acid, pH about 2–3; it etches and permanently damages calcareous stone on contact. Lemon is citric, same damage. CLR is highly acidic and causes severe etching. Commercial bathroom descalers are almost all acids.

Do not use vinegar “just for a second.” Soapstone Werks and Rockstar Granite skip vinegar on soapstone. Standing water leaves minerals. Acid is still not the plan. NSI: lemon, vinegar, or other acids may dull or etch calcareous stone. The dull patch after acid is a hole in the finish or the oil. Cleaning will not fill it.

Never mix leftover vinegar or a rust remover with bleach. Poison Control: mixing bleach with an acid, including vinegar, forms chlorine gas. Washington State DOH names vinegar as one of those acids, and rust-removal products too. Leave the area and breathe fresh air if you already mixed them. Poison Help **1-800-222-1222**.
