The CLEAN light stays on because you ran water, skipped a side, or never started the flush on the machine’s own clock. DualBrew CFP200 and DualBrew Pro CFP300 print it in one line: **a water-only clean cycle will not descale the brewer properly.** CLEAN is a reminder to run a descale *mix*, not tap water. After the mix, those sheets want a water-only *brew* — Full Carafe Classic on grounds, a 12 oz Classic on pods — not a second water-only CLEAN. Do not copy the [Keurig](/articles/how-to-descale-a-keurig-with-vinegar) rinse count. Ninja’s clock is its own.

## What you are dissolving

USGS: hardness is dissolved calcium and magnesium. Heat them and you get calcium carbonate — scale — on the heater. USGS names the home coffee maker. DualBrew Pro: the orange Intelligent Clean Cycle Indicator is a reminder; ignore it and the brewer can stop mid-brew and show **CLn** on the current mode. CLn is heavy scale on *that* path, not a hardness sensor. Hard-water houses see it sooner. [Test the tap](/articles/how-to-test-water-hardness-at-home) if you want the number.

Empty the carafe. Pull the paper or permanent coffee filter and any pod. DualBrew CFP200 / CFP300 and Specialty CM401 sheets we fetched do **not** list an in-tank charcoal water-filter holder. If *your* QSG names one, pull it before acid.

## Three bottles. Check *your* ounces.

DualBrew, DualBrew Pro, Specialty CM401, and Hot & Iced (CM360 / CM371) still print **white vinegar by the ounce**: **16 oz** to the Travel Mug line, then water to Full (or Max Fill). DualBrew Full is **60 oz**. Specialty Max Fill is **50 oz**. Hot & Iced Full is **50 oz or 60 oz** (CM360 is 50). Same ounce line does not mean one button combo.

## DualBrew: grounds path, then pod path

CFP200 DualBrew is **two modes: Grounds and Pods**. Each mode you use needs its own clean cycle. CFP300 DualBrew Pro adds **Hot Water** on some models — a separate water line, independent of coffee. If you use hot water at all, Pro wants an extra cycle there. Do not merge the sides.

First orange light: the mode you use most. Grounds: sliding lid closed. Pods: Ninja Pod Adapter installed, run through the adapter. Next orange light: the remaining coffee path. **CLn**: the *current* mode.

Quoted from the DualBrew / DualBrew Pro owner’s guides:

1. Empty carafe under the brew basket.
2. [Urnex Dezcal](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B003PSJ7F8?tag=hardwaterfi04-20) or another coffeemaker descaler to the Full line, per the package. **OR** Travel Mug line (**16 oz**) white vinegar, then water to Full. **Only use white vinegar.**
3. Press **CLEAN**, then **START BREW** to confirm. About **75 minutes**. Small shots, long pauses. Do not pull the carafe. Cancel (Power or CLEAN once) and the sheet says the brewer **will not be properly descaled** — rinse what ran, then start the mix cycle again.
4. Beep, clock shows **End**, orange indicator off.
5. Dump the carafe. Wash the reservoir with warm soapy water.
6. Fresh water to Full. Water-only flush: **Full Carafe Classic** (Grounds); **12 oz Classic** (Pods); **Full Carafe on BOIL** (Hot Water on Pro, not all models). Rerun that water brew if taste persists.

That **12 oz** is a cup size on the pod path. It is not twelve rinses.

Then the other path you actually use — same mix, same CLEAN → START BREW clock.

## Programmable drip: Specialty and Hot & Iced

CM401 Specialty and Hot & Iced still use the **16 oz** vinegar fill. They do **not** use DualBrew’s two-button start.

- **CM401 Specialty:** Full Carafe size, empty carafe. Descaler to **Max Fill 50 oz**, or 16 oz vinegar then water to Max Fill. Press **CLEAN** only. About **60 minutes**. End, light off. Soapy reservoir. Fresh water to Max Fill. Flush: **Full Carafe, Classic** water brew. CLEAN light back on: the sheet says calcium is still there — repeat from the beginning.
- **Hot & Iced CM360 / CM371:** same 16 oz vinegar (Full **50 oz / 60 oz**). Press **CLEAN**. About **60–75 minutes**. Then a Full Carafe Classic water brew.

Espresso (Luxe Café) is a third family: DESCALE light, portafilter, [Ninja descaling solution](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DV9XWNZW?tag=hardwaterfi04-20) packets. Not this CLEAN button. Open that QSG.

## Why the light stays on

- **Water-only CLEAN.** DualBrew: it will not descale. Mix in the tank *before* CLEAN. The post-flush is a **water brew**, not CLEAN with tap water.
- **Skipped side.** Grounds does not clean Pods. Pro Hot Water is a third.
- **Aborted cycle.** DualBrew: cancel = not descaled. Rinse, then let the mix countdown hit End.
- **Wrong start.** DualBrew needs **CLEAN then START BREW**. Specialty and Hot & Iced need **CLEAN** alone.
- **Filter or pod still in.** Empty brew basket. No pod. Water-filter holder out if *your* sheet names one.
- **Hard water, one pass not enough.** DualBrew troubleshooting: orange after a recent clean → another mix cycle. CM401: light back on → repeat the whole mix cycle.

## What not to do

- Do not pour CLR, Bar Keepers Friend, Lime-A-Way, or dishwasher rinse aid into a brew path.
- Do not mix any descaler with bleach. Do not use apple cider.
- Do not run one ounce count and one button combo across DualBrew, Specialty, Hot & Iced, and espresso.

Kettle scale is a different machine: [how to descale an electric kettle](/articles/how-to-descale-an-electric-kettle).

## FAQ

**Does a water-only CLEAN descale?**
No. DualBrew CFP200 / DualBrew Pro CFP300: a water-only clean cycle will not descale the brewer properly. Mix first. Water brew after.

**I descaled Grounds. The light came back on a pod.**
Expected. Each DualBrew mode you use needs its own cycle. CLn is the current path.

**Vinegar, Dezcal, or Ninja’s bottle?**
If *your* ounces are in the PDF, grocery white vinegar is the manual path. [Dezcal](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B003PSJ7F8?tag=hardwaterfi04-20) when the booklet allows a coffeemaker descaling solution. [Ninja descaling solution](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DV9XWNZW?tag=hardwaterfi04-20) when *your* sheet names Ninja’s own (espresso packets are that branded SKU). DualBrew sheets we fetched do not name it.

**Does vinegar void the Ninja warranty?**
We will not invent that sentence. DualBrew, Specialty, and Hot & Iced guides we fetched still list white vinegar. In-warranty: follow the booklet in the box.

**How often?**
When the orange CLEAN light or CLn says so. DualBrew: hard water asks more often. A reminder light is not a hardness meter.
