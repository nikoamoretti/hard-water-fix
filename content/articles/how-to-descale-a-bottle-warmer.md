2. Empty leftover warming water. Remove the bottle, jar, basket, or lifter. Soft wipe loose flakes. Do not start acid on top of milk residue or heavy sludge — wash food bits first with mild soap and a soft sponge, then rinse.
3. Mix vinegar and water **exactly as YOUR sheet prints**. Avent-style example: **60 ml distilled white vinegar (5% pantry) + 120 ml cold water** (**1:2**). Other sheets may print a different measured fill or a 1:1 line — match yours. Pour into the warmer chamber only. Do not overfill so the mix floods the exterior, knob, or cord.
4. Run the **descale / milk-warming cycle** the booklet names (Avent-style: often the milk setting for contents under 180 ml / 6 oz, until auto-off or about 10 minutes). Stay in the room. 6. Soft cloth or soft sponge on the chamber floor, the fill marks, and the walls. No green pad. No steel wool. No powdered cleanser in a baby-path chamber.
7. Rinse the chamber with clean water until there is **no vinegar smell**. Baby path — rinse until the chamber smells like water, not pickle. Repeat rinses as needed.
8. Optional **one plain-water warm cycle after**. Fill with plain water (prefer pantry distilled for this rinse if the tap is hard). Run one short empty warm. Smell the chamber. Water only. Discard that water. Dry before the next bottle.
