CLEAN on a Hamilton Beach is scale on the heater path, not a dirty glass pot. BrewStation 47900 / 47950 (840213202): **"CLEAN" appears in display** is a reminder to clean the coffeemaker. Cycle timer: every 30 cycles, CLEAN flashes for 2 cycles. Residue sensor: CLEAN stays on until you descale. The coffee tank is inside. You cannot see that scale the way you see film on a carafe.

This is not a [Ninja](/articles/how-to-descale-ninja-coffee-maker) CLEAN clock, a [Cuisinart](/articles/how-to-descale-cuisinart-coffee-maker) five-beep hold, or a [Keurig vinegar](/articles/how-to-descale-a-keurig-with-vinegar) bottle path. Open *your* booklet.

USGS: hardness is dissolved calcium and magnesium. Heat them and you get calcium carbonate — scale — in a home coffee maker. Vinegar charges those particles so they rinse. Hard at 121 mg/L as CaCO3; very hard above 180. [Test the tap](/articles/how-to-test-water-hardness-at-home) if you want the number. Hamilton Beach publishes vinegar. Amounts are not one recipe.

## Pull the charcoal filter. Water-only is not a descale.

If the machine has the optional charcoal water filter, pull it before acid. Drip 840153800: **Remove water filter and water filter holder (optional feature).** BrewStation 840213202: **Remove optional water filter.** Then pour. The filter is not a descale.

A water-only brew does not descale. First-use water flushes are for taste, not scale. FlexBrew 49940 (840274402): **BEFORE FIRST USE: Brew one cycle with water on each side and discard water.** That is not a descale.

## Drip and FlexBrew — quote *your* sheet

Do not average ½ cup and 1 pint.

**2-Way FlexBrew 49983 / 49984C / 49985C (840219703).** Quote: **Pour 1/2 cup (118 ml) of plain white vinegar and 1/2 cup (118 ml) of cold water into water reservoir.** Press I (ON). Press OFF after 30 seconds and wait 30 minutes. Press ON. Finish. **Run 2–3 more brew cycles with CLEAN WATER only.** Then **repeat process for single-serve side.** Canada's how-to prints the same ½ + ½ for FlexBrew carafe and drip.

**The Scoop 2-Way (840231300)** splits the sides. Single-serve: **Pour 1/2 cup (125 ml) of plain white vinegar and 1/2 cup (125 ml) of cold water into single-serve side reservoir.** Same 30-second / 30-minute pause. Carafe: **Pour 1 pint (2 cups/500 ml) vinegar into carafe side reservoir.** Then 2–3 CLEAN WATER cycles per side. **The unit will only brew on one side at a time.**

**2-Way FlexBrew 49940 / 49950 / 49956 / 49976 (840274402)** is a different fill. Carafe: **Pour 1 pint (2 cups/473 ml) of plain white vinegar into the carafe water reservoir.** 30 seconds, stop, 30 minutes, finish. **Run 2 to 3 cycles of cold tap water.** Single-serve: **Pour 1 cup (273 ml) of plain white vinegar into the single-serve water reservoir.** 14-oz. cup underneath. Press BREW NOW. **When solution starts dispensing,** stop. Wait 30 minutes. Finish. Then 2 to 3 cold tap water cycles. The US blog matches that 2-Way: 1 pint carafe, 1 cup single-serve.

**FlexBrew Single-Serve 49903 (840353300):** **Pour 3/4 cup (177 ml) of plain white vinegar and 3/4 cup (177 ml) of cold water into Reservoir.** 30 seconds, 30 minutes, finish. **Run 2 to 3 cycles of cold tap water.**

**Programmable drip (840153800):** Pull the filter. Press ON. After **20 seconds,** press OFF. **Pour one pint of plain white vinegar into the reservoir.** Wait **20-30 minutes.** Turn on again. Cool. Empty. **Pour one carafe of cold tap water.** Brew. Cool. **Repeat this step with one more carafe of cold tap water.** The US drip blog also prints **2 cups (1 pint)**, then 30 seconds / 30 minutes, then 2–3 clean-water cycles. Canada's drip how-to prints ½ + ½. Use the PDF for *your* model.

## BrewStation — 1 pint, 20 seconds

BrewStation is a dispenser with an internal coffee tank. CLEAN is scale in that tank and the heater, not a dirty carafe.

**47900 / 47950 (840213202):** **Remove optional water filter. Pour 1 pint (2 cups/500 ml) vinegar into water reservoir.** **Press ON. Turn OFF after 20 seconds and wait 30 minutes. Press ON. Turn OFF when finished.** When cool, **dispense several cups before emptying and rinsing reservoir.** Then **run 2–3 more brew cycles with CLEAN WATER only.** Monthly; weekly in hard water.

Older BrewStation 840136100 is not the same clock. After 20 seconds, turn OFF. **Wait 20 minutes** (not 30). Then turn ON. Rinse: **one coffee tank of cold tap water,** then **two or more coffee tanks of cold water.** Do not copy 47950's 30-minute soak onto this 20-minute soak.

## Optional cleaner (no invented ASIN)

Vinegar is the published pantry path. Drip 840153800 also names **Cleaner and Descaler (part #990024700)** and notes commercially-available brands include **CleanCaf**. Follow the packet. After cleaner: **thoroughly rinse coffeemaker by brewing twice with water only.** BrewStation 47900/47950 prefers cleaner over vinegar (more effective, no odor). Optional class: Hamilton Beach Cleaner and Descaler part #990024700 or CleanCaf. Not Dezcal, Keurig bottle, Breville powder, or citric. ## Rinse water

Rinse from the booklet you used. 840153800, 840274402, and 840353300 print **cold tap water**. 840219703, 840231300, and BrewStation 840213202 print **CLEAN WATER**. No fetched booklet names distilled as a rinse. Brew tips (840274402, 840219703, 840231300): **Using a water filter, filtered water, or bottled water will yield better tasting coffee than tap water.** BrewStation 840213202: **Use water filter or bottled water to make coffee.** That is brew water, not a rinse SKU.

## Two FlexBrew sides. The carafe is a different job.

If the machine has a carafe side and a single-serve side, descale both if you use both. 840219703: **The unit will only brew on one side at a time.** Finish one side, including rinses, then repeat. 840274402 same rule. The carafe side does not clean the pod needle.

White film on the glass or stainless pot is the carafe, not the heater. Wash that pot on [how to remove hard water stains from a coffee carafe](/articles/how-to-remove-hard-water-stains-from-coffee-carafe). That soak is not a machine descale.

A [Breville espresso](/articles/how-to-descale-breville-espresso-machine) wants branded powder. Different brand. Do not copy those steps.

## What not to do

- Do not mash ½ cup + ½ cup with 1 pint. Quote the model booklet.
- Do not mash BrewStation **20 seconds** with FlexBrew **30 seconds**, or 840136100's **20-minute** soak with 47950's **30-minute** soak.
- Do not leave the charcoal filter in. Do not treat a water-only brew as a descale.
- Do not skip the unused FlexBrew side if you brew on it.
- Do not pour CLR, bleach, or dishwasher detergent into the reservoir.
- Do not copy a Keurig rinse count, a Ninja DualBrew clock, or a Cuisinart ⅓-vinegar mix onto this maker.
- Do not invent a Hamilton Beach descaler ASIN. Do not dishwasher the BrewStation tank (840213202).

## FAQ

**½ cup or 1 pint?**
Both. FlexBrew 49983-series and Canada's drip how-to: ½ cup vinegar + ½ cup cold water. FlexBrew 49940 carafe, Scoop carafe, drip 840153800, BrewStation 47900/47950, and the US blog: 1 pint. Open the PDF.

**30-second pause?**
Printed on FlexBrew sheets and both brand how-tos: 30 seconds, stop, 30 minutes, finish. BrewStation 47900/47950: **20 seconds**, then 30 minutes. Older 840136100: 20 seconds, then **20 minutes**.

**Do I descale both FlexBrew sides?**
Yes if you use both. One side at a time.

**Can I use distilled to rinse?**
No booklet we fetched names distilled as a rinse. They print cold tap water or CLEAN WATER. For brewing: tap, charcoal filter, filtered, or bottled — not distilled as the daily fill.

**Is CLEAN a dirty carafe?**
No. BrewStation 840213202: CLEAN is a reminder to clean the coffeemaker. The tank is internal.
