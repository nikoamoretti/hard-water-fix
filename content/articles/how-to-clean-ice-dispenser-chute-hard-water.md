## Vinegar 1:1. Soft nylon brush or spray in the chute. Rinse. Dry.

## Hard-water signs in an ice dispenser chute

Know the film before you open the vinegar.

- Faint white haze on the flap lip after melt dries: early mineral film. Short 1:1 spray and a dry wipe stretch the gap.
- Raised chalk a fingernail catches inside the bore: carbonate scale on the delivery path. Soften. Soft nylon brush. Rinse. Dry.
- Cubes that stick or grind on the way out with white rings on the chute wall: packed scale, not “weak ice.”
- Cloudy spots that return overnight after a successful clean: rinse water flash-dried — dry habit failed, not “vinegar failed.”
- Pink or black slime with a musty smell: hygiene / biofilm first. Clean mold per the fridge booklet, then treat leftover mineral film with vinegar. Do not bleach into the chute as a hard-water fix.
