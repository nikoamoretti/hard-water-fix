1. Confirm the part is the **shower door handle / lever**, not the hinge, not a faucet, not a grab bar. Wipe hair and grit off the grip dry.
2. **Soap scum first.** A drop of dish soap on a damp soft cloth. Wipe the grip, the rose, and the underside. Soap film is greasy; it shields chalk from acid. Rinse the soap off with a clean damp cloth before any vinegar wrap.
3. Identify the finish. Bare chrome, stainless, brushed nickel, PVD, matte black, oil-rubbed bronze, painted, coated. CLR stays off PVD, matte, specialty, brass, painted, and any finish the sheet forbids. See the CLR section below.
4. Soak a soft cloth in pantry distilled white vinegar (5% grocery bottle). Equal parts water — **50/50** — is enough for routine haze. Straight vinegar is fine on raised crust if you stay in the room. **Wrap the handle / lever.** Cover the grip, the rose face, and the underside. Tuck the cloth into the set-screw pocket if you can reach it without prying the glass. Rubber-band the wrap if it slips.
5. **Short wrap.** Stay in the room. **10–15 minutes max** on chrome or stainless. Re-wet the cloth if it dries. This is a timed cloth wrap, **not** an overnight vinegar sock. Do not walk away and leave acid on the lever. Do not wrap the hinge. Do not wrap a grab bar as this job.
6. Unwrap. **Soft toothbrush** in the crevices — rose edge, set-screw pocket, lever underside, and the ring where the handle meets the glass. Small circles. No metal pick. No grout saw. No green pad on chrome.
7. **Distilled pantry rinse.** Grocery jug, pantry text. Wipe or rinse until it does not smell like pickle. No Amazon distilled URL (NONE_QUALIFY). Never `B07VD4KN28`. Never `B07VC5RZHM`. A tap rinse on very hard water can drop a fresh film on the same lever you just cleaned.
8. **Dry fully.** Soft towel the grip, the rose, the crevices, and the glass ring. Leave no damp film. Spots return if you skip the dry.

One soap wipe plus one vinegar cloth wrap clears most fresh scum-plus-chalk. Raised crust that survives two short wraps on **bare chrome or stainless** the finish sheet allows can take the optional CLR step below. Vinegar will not resurrect PVD that already dulled, a peeling powder coat, or a rusted-through cheap plating — stop acid and replace the handle if the finish is failing.

Never mix leftover vinegar with bleach. Poison Control: bleach plus an acid, including vinegar, forms chlorine gas. Leave the area. Fresh air. Poison Help **1-800-222-1222**. If bleach was ever used on the handle, rinse with plain water until it does not smell like bleach before any vinegar wrap.
