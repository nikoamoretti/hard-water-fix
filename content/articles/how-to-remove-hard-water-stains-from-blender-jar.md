## Hard-water signs on a blender jar

Know the stain before you open the vinegar.

- Faint white haze on the walls after the jar air-dries: early mineral film. Overnight vinegar soak and a dry habit stretch the gap.
- Raised white chalk a fingernail catches near the blade hub or waterline: carbonate scale. ~1 cup vinegar + warm water. Hours or overnight. Soft sponge near blades. Rinse. Dry.
- Permanent cloudy look that does not soften after two overnight vinegar soaks on plastic: likely etch or scratch haze, not removable mineral. Honesty — it may not clear.
- Berry purple, turmeric yellow, or green tint that soap does not fully lift: food color stain. Soap and time (sometimes UV). Not vinegar’s main mineral job.
- Greasy film that smells like last night’s smoothie: food film. Dish soap first. Then vinegar for any chalk left underneath.
