## Assemble the empty stem and basket

The acid has to perk. A puddle in the pot does not clean the pump.

West Bend 54159 (ManualsLib): assemble with **stem, basket, spreader, and cover**. Place the basket onto the stem and insert them into the heating unit well. Seat the stem in the well so the liquid can perk. Cuisinart PRC-12: place the basket and pump tube so the bottom of the tube fits into the well. Be sure that all parts are in place, lid securely on. Presto: the perk well is the indented portion in the center of the coffee maker base. If the perk tube is not seated in that well, it will not make coffee.

Empty basket. No grounds. Cookwithrome electric: assemble without coffee grounds and run a full brew cycle.

Unplug an electric pot first. Cool completely. West Bend: unplug from the outlet and disconnect the cord. Allow the percolator to cool completely before cleaning.

## Brown is oil. White is mineral.

Look at the film in daylight.

**Brown, tan, or a rainbow sheen** that soap cuts is coffee oil. Coletti: coffee oils stick to metal and turn rancid. That is dish soap, a soft sponge, and a rinse. Farberware: an accumulation of coffee oil or stains can impair the taste; that sheet’s oil play is dishwashing detergent in the basket, percolate 5 minutes. Presto: periodically remove oil, stains, and any sediment with 3 tablespoons of automatic dishwashing detergent in the basket, not vinegar. That is oil. It is not this page’s mineral product.

**White, chalky crust** that soap does not move is mineral. USGS: vinegar (an acid) charges those particles so water can carry them away. Farberware splits the two: a spotted white film from hard water is a vinegar or lemon-juice sponge; mineral deposits inside the pot are the equal-parts vinegar simmer. Presto: accumulated sediment in the perk well negatively affects the perking action. White crust in that well is the vinegar perk, not more soap.

Both films: soap the brown first. Then vinegar on the white. Do not mix vinegar and baking soda in the pot. The fizz is CO2, not extra scale power.

Yes. Empty. West Bend: stem, basket, spreader, cover, stem seated in the well. Cuisinart: all parts in place, pump tube in the well. No grounds.
