## Hard-water signs on a towel warmer

Know the stain before you open the vinegar.

- Faint white haze on the bars after splash dries: early mineral film. Short 50/50 wipe and a dry habit stretch the gap.
- Raised white chalk a fingernail catches on bars or joints: carbonate scale. Cool first. Soften 50/50 short contact. Soft cloth. Rinse. Dry.
- Streaks that appear right after a wipe on a still-warm rail: you wiped too soon. Cool fully. Wipe again. Dry.
- Orange on cheap plated steel: rust, not this calcium path. Stop acid if plating is failing through. Replace a rusted-through rail. Do not invent a replacement ASIN on this page.
