## Hard-water signs on an aquarium canopy

Know the film before you open the vinegar.

- Faint white dots on the underside after splash and condensation dry: early mineral film. Remove the cover. Vinegar **1:1** off-tank wipe or short soak. Soft cloth. Distilled or dechlorinated rinse. Dry.
- Raised white chalk a fingernail catches on the pane or drip lip: carbonate scale. Same off-tank vinegar path. Plastic scraper. Optional [CLR](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C4G2BJKX?tag=hardwaterfi04-20) **off-tank only** on allowed glass, two-minute clock, rinse until no residue.

- Cloudy acrylic that stays cloudy after chalk is gone: etched or scratched plastic. Honesty — acid clears mineral; it does not polish acrylic clear again.
