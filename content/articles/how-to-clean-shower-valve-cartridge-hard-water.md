## Shut off. Cover the drain. Photograph orientation.

1. Shut off the shower supplies or the house main. Open the shower to bleed pressure.
2. Cover the drain with a rag or plug so a dropped clip, spring, or screw does not vanish.
3. Pull the handle and trim per the brand sheet. Photograph the stem orientation — notch, hot/cold mark, flat — before the cartridge leaves the body.
4. Remove the retaining clip or nut the booklet shows. Pull the cartridge straight. Do not twist a pressure-balance spool like a screw unless the sheet says so.
5. If the valve body is full of grit, flush the open ports into a bucket **before** you put a clean cartridge back. Moen-style M-Core service often wants that flush when mineral or debris is the complaint.

Posi-Temp / pressure-balance vs thermostatic: different cartridges, different rebuild kits. Quote the series on the valve or the old cartridge stamp. Guessing the wrong Moen number is how you buy the wrong part twice.

## Pantry vinegar 50/50 — soak the removed cartridge

Distilled white vinegar. Grocery bottle. Pantry. No Amazon vinegar URL. Distilled water for a rinse, if you use it, is pantry text only — no Amazon gallon link on this page.

**Read the sheet for your valve before any soak.** Brand care (Ask This Old House–style cartridge cleans, Bristan hard-water vinegar notes, Sigma-style 1:1 vinegar windows) points at a **removed** cartridge in a cup — not acid poured into the open wall cavity as a default.

1. Mix pantry distilled white vinegar and water **50/50** in a cup or jar big enough to cover the cartridge ports and spool.
2. Soak the **removed** cartridge. Typical window: about 1–3 hours for heavy crust when the maker’s pattern is a vinegar de-scale — follow a shorter clock if **your** booklet prints one. Soft toothbrush on ports and screens. No steel wool. No scouring powder on plastic or rubber.
3. Rinse thoroughly. Pantry distilled rinse on the metal/plastic body if you want zero redeposit from tap rinse water.
4. Inspect: a rattling Delta-style cartridge that cleaned up can go back. A scored Moen 1222 spool, cracked plastic, or shredded rubber seat is a **replace**, not another soak.
5. Light **silicone faucet grease** on O-rings and seals the sheet names. Hand-seat the cartridge in the photographed orientation. Clip or nut back. Trim on. Supplies on. Check mix and shutoff.

**Do not overnight-soak rubber pressure-balance seats or spools in acid as a lazy default.** Rubber and long acid sits do not mix. Vinegar 50/50 on the removed cartridge for the booklet window — then rinse.

## Keep it from packing again

After a clean pass, exercise the handle through hot and cold once a week if your tap is hard so the spool does not sit in one crusted position. If the house is very hard, a softener or point-of-use filter is the long game — this page does not sell one.

When you deep-clean the head, remember the valve exists. Do not wait until the shower scalds on a pressure drop or the handle will not turn.
