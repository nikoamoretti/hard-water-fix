## Pull the drawer. Quote your booklet for the release tab.

The drawer sits in a housing on the front of an HE/front-load washer. Electrolux: ease it forward until it stops, release a button lock, then slide it fully out. On some machines the release sits on the side of the drawer. AppliancePartsPros (Frigidaire/Kenmore front-load): pull the drawer out as far as it will go. A small tab. Push that tab down. The drawer comes the rest of the way. Haier: pull the drawer open until it stops. Press the release button and remove the drawer. Haier: review the manual before yanking too hard on the wrong part. The tab is not one shape. Quote **your** booklet. Do not yank past the stop.

1. Unplug the washer. Do not skip this. Water and a live machine do not mix.
2. Pull the drawer until it stops. That stop is the lock, not a jam.
3. Find the release tab. Booklet first. Press and hold. Pull straight. Do not twist the rails.
4. Photograph the inserts. They go back the same way.
5. Lift out the fabric-softener siphon cover and any bleach insert. Electrolux: remove the fabric-conditioner section lid and clean the siphon tube so conditioner can leave the cup.
6. Look up. The housing ceiling has spray jets. That is the next job, not optional.

Electrolux: the drawer and the inside and underside of the dispenser housing need cleaning periodically — once every two months, or more often if used extensively. Haier: at least once a month.

## Vinegar soak of the pulled drawer — 30–60 minutes

**Pulled drawer only.** Distilled white vinegar. Grocery bottle. Pantry. No Amazon link.

## Brush the housing jets or it clogs again

Electrolux: particular attention to the top underside of the drawer opening — the “shower holes” that supply water to the dispenser. The bristles of a brush clear buildup from around this area. Haier: do not forget the top part of the compartment.

1. Flashlight. Look up at the ceiling of the housing.
2. Stiff nylon brush. Electrolux: a suitable stiff nylon brush. Brush the jets until you can see holes.
3. Wipe the rails and the floor of the recess. Electrolux: inside and underside of the dispenser housing.
4. If a jet is still a white pin, wooden toothpick. Not a nail. Not a screw.

Skip this and the next load dumps powder back onto a crusted ceiling. The drawer comes out clean and clogs again in a week.
