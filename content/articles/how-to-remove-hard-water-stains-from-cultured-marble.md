## Name it first. Gel coat is not a slab.

Cultured marble is polyester resin plus marble dust under a gel coat. ARSTAR: crushed natural marble bound in polyester resin, then a pigmented gel coat. House Cleaning Central: about 75% marble dust and 25% resin, then a gel coating that makes it durable. Engineer Fix: the gel coat is the waterproof, high-gloss skin. That layer is what you are cleaning.

It is not calcite slab. Real marble is calcium carbonate. Vinegar etches it. That job lives on [how to remove hard water stains from marble](/articles/how-to-remove-hard-water-stains-from-marble). Do not copy that no-vinegar ladder onto this coat. Do not copy this vinegar cloth onto that slab.

Identify the piece before you wet it.

- One-piece vanity bowl and deck, same color, no sink seam: cultured. Complete Home Concepts: integrated sink and backsplash, thin edge, flat dull underside. ARSTAR: the bowl and surface are cast as one piece. The gel coat never needs sealing. Gloss is repairable. No sealer on a calendar.
- Separate undermount bowl, seams, sealer on a calendar: real marble or granite. Granite is [how to remove hard water stains from granite](/articles/how-to-remove-hard-water-stains-from-granite). Still no vinegar there.
- Hollow, flexy stall that yellows: fiberglass. ARSTAR vs FRP: fiberglass is the economy shell, 5–10 years, basic gloss. Do not merge it here. Do not invent a fiberglass URL. Brilliant Bath stays off this page.

1. Mix equal parts distilled white vinegar and water. Gloves. Open a window or run the fan.
2. Soak a soft cloth. Wring so it is wet, not dripping a pool into the bowl.
3. Lay it on the crust only. 15–30 minutes. Re-wet if it starts to dry.
4. Wipe with the same soft cloth. No scouring pad. No steel wool.
5. Rinse. Dry with a second cloth.

## Wear vs crust. Dull after cleaning is not mineral.

Raised, chalky, a fingernail catches: crust on top of the gel coat. The vinegar cloth is for that.

Smooth and dull after the crust is gone: gel-coat wear. Micro-scratches, old powder, a Magic Eraser habit, or acid left too long. Cleaning will not polish that back. A cloth does not refill gloss. ARSTAR: light scratches on gloss buff with automotive wax. That wax is cosmetic. It is not a mineral remover. It does not undo a worn coat.

Do the feel test before you add more acid.

1. Rinse. Dry.
2. Feel it with a fingernail.
3. Raised and chalky: another 15–30 minute cloth. Stop at 30.
4. Smooth and dull, same wet and dry: stop. That is the coat, not leftover scale.

ARSTAR: rubbing compound is too aggressive for the gel coat. Powdered cleanser, steel wool, and Magic Eraser as a habit frost the same layer. Gulf States Marble names a Magic Eraser for mineral and soap scum. This page does not make that a habit. Engineer Fix: scouring powders and abrasive pads create micro-scratches that dull the finish.

**Can I use vinegar on cultured marble?**

Yes, as a timed cloth. ARSTAR care: 50/50 white vinegar and water, 15–30 minutes, then wipe. Not a puddle. Not daily soap. Daily is mild soap and a soft cloth.

**Can I use CLR?**
