The DESCALE light is scale in the heater path, not a dirty drip tray. Breville: hard water leaves mineral build-up on inner parts and cuts brew flow, temperature, power, and taste. Pull the tank filter if your booklet names one. Use a labeled espresso powder — not vinegar. Each US model uses a different button combo.

## What that light is

USGS: hardness is dissolved calcium and magnesium. Heat them and you get calcium carbonate — scale — on heaters. USGS names the home coffee maker.

Barista Express (BES870): **CLEAN / DESCALE solid on** means descale. **Flashing** is a tablet back-flush, a different cycle. Barista Pro (BES878): LCD **DESCALE**. Barista Touch (BES880): a pop-up. Bambino (BES450): **2 Cup** flashes continuously. Bambino Plus (BES500): **1 CUP**, **STEAM**, and **2 CUP** flash alternately for 15 seconds. Bambino and Plus: pressing a button can **cancel the alert** and return to ready. That is not a descale.

Do not copy a [Ninja CLEAN](/articles/how-to-descale-ninja-coffee-maker) or [Keurig-without-vinegar](/articles/how-to-descale-keurig-without-vinegar) rinse count onto a Breville boiler.

## What to use

Current US books for these five models name **descaling powder** or a **descaling agent** — [Breville’s Machine Descaler](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GQ51XV9R?tag=hardwaterfi04-20) is the labeled sachet those books mean. They do not print a vinegar boiler recipe. Sage (Breville’s UK brand): vinegar may harm rubber seals and gaskets, can leave taste, and using it instead of a descaler may void warranty. Urnex: vinegar is harder to rinse, slower, and leaves odor and aftertaste.

[Breville descaling powder](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GQ51XV9R?tag=hardwaterfi04-20) is the named-kit path (US: the Machine Descaler™; older Descaler pack still listed for BES870 and BES500). Dezcal is Urnex’s coffee-industry powder for espresso machines and boilers. Same job: a labeled acid for the tank. Not a ranking.

Urnex ingredients: citric acid is the main cleaning agent (plus sulfamic). Food-grade citric is the same acid family. We are not linking a grocery jar. No Instant bottle. Vinegar on an Instant Pot is the [inner pot only](/articles/how-to-descale-instant-pot) — not this boiler.

## Prep

Empty the drip tray. Mix per **that** product’s label, then follow **that** model’s tank line. Do not average the fills.

Urnex home packet: **one 28 g packet in 32 oz (1 L)** lukewarm; espresso: follow the machine. Express US: **one [Breville](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GQ51XV9R?tag=hardwaterfi04-20) sachet into 34 fl oz (1 L)**, then fill the tank. Pro, Bambino, Plus: fill to the tank **DESCALE** line, then add the agent. Touch: the screen runs the mix.

**Filter.** Pro: “Ensure that the water filter is removed from the water tank before adding the descaling powder.” ANZ Express prints the same NOTE. US Express V23 descale block starts at the sachet and does **not** reprint that sentence — still pull a tank filter if you have one; Pro/ANZ are why. Put it back after the rinse. Bambino / Plus booklets we fetched do not list an in-tank filter. Touch: follow the screen; that Descale paragraph does not print the Pro filter-out line.

Tank: water plus labeled descaler only. No CLR, no Iron Out, no bleach. Housing: a damp cloth. [CLR vs Iron Out](/articles/clr-vs-iron-out) is a surface split, not a boiler fill.

Breville on these books: do **not** use distilled or demineralized water as the everyday tank fill (taste and how the machine is designed to function). Touch also: never distilled or low-mineral water; hardness 4–5 → change source.

### Barista Express (BES870) — US V23

1. Confirm CLEAN/DESCALE is **solid**. Flashing is the tablet cycle; use that page of the book instead.
2. Dissolve one Breville sachet in 34 fl oz (1 L) and fill the tank. Filter already out if the tank has one (Pro/ANZ NOTE).
3. Hold **2-CUP**, then hold **POWER**. POWER flashes while it heats. Ready when POWER, 1 CUP, CLEAN/DESCALE, and HOT WATER/STEAM are all solid.
4. Press **1-CUP** for the coffee component (~25 seconds). Press again within 25 seconds only to stop it.
5. Steam: dial **RIGHT** (~13 seconds). Hot water: dial **LEFT** (~8 seconds). Dial STANDBY stops a steam/hot-water pass early.
6. About half (17 fl oz / 0.5 L) remains. Repeat steps 4–6 to empty the solution.
7. Rinse: tank out, rinse, fill to **MAX 68 fl oz / 2 L**. Repeat steps 4–6.
8. Press **POWER** to quit. Idle 5 minutes in descale mode and it powers off; resume from step 3.

### Barista Pro (BES878)

1. Empty drip tray. Tank off. **Remove the filter.**
2. Fill to the **DESCALE** line. Add the agent. Reattach.
3. **MENU** → GRIND AMOUNT to **DESCALE** → press. 1 CUP lights.
4. Press **1 CUP**. Three stages: coffee, hot water, steam. Beep after each. Turn the steam dial when the LCD says so.
5. When descale is done, 1 CUP lights again — the book says the machine **needs a rinse cycle**. Same steam-dial prompts.
6. Empty remaining descaling liquid. Fill to **MAX**. Press **1 CUP** for the 3-stage rinse.
7. Beep, **READY**. Rinse tray and tank. Filter back after the rinse.

### Barista Touch (BES880)

The US book does not print a button-hold recipe:

1. Cool at least **1 hour**. Drip tray empty and inserted. Caution: hot steam.
2. **Settings → Descale cycle**. Follow the instructions **on the screen**.
3. Do not remove or completely empty the tank during descaling.

Do not guess Express’s 2-CUP combo.

### Bambino (BES450)

1. Empty drip tray; re-insert.
2. Fill to the **DESCALE** line. Add the agent.
3. 2 L / 68 fl oz container under the group head **and** steam wand.
4. Press **2 Cup** and **Steam** together for **5 seconds**.
5. When the solution is used up, the mode **pauses**. Rinse and fill to **MAX**. Empty the waste container.
6. Press **2 Cup** for the rinse cycle. Ready when it finishes. Rinse tray and tank.

“Press any button” only clears the flashing alert.

### Bambino Plus (BES500)

1. Empty drip tray. Fill to the **DESCALE** line. Add the agent.
2. 2 L / 68 fl oz container under the portafilter **and** steam wand.
3. Turn **off** with **1 CUP + STEAM**. Let it **completely cool**.
4. Still off: **1 CUP + STEAM** for **5 seconds**. Both illuminate when ready. Press **1 CUP** or **STEAM** to begin.
5. When the solution is used up, pause. Refill to **MAX**. Empty the waste container.
6. Press **1 CUP** or **STEAM** to **resume** (this book does not title that pass “rinse cycle”). Complete when 1 CUP and STEAM flash, then standby. Rinse tray and tank.

If 1 CUP and 2 CUP are flashing, that is the **cleaning cycle** (tablet + disc), not this descale.

## Rinse / why the light stays on

Pro: after the acid pass, “the machine needs to complete a rinse cycle” before READY. Express: a full 2 L repeat of 4–6, then POWER to quit. Bambino: press 2 Cup for a named rinse. Plus: refill MAX and resume. Skip that pass and you did not finish. Do not invent a 17-flush Keurig clone.

Bambino / Plus: cancelling the flashing alert also turns lights off. That is not a completed cycle.

## What not to do

- Do not pour vinegar into these boilers. Current books name powder/agent. Sage: seals, taste, possible warranty issue.
- Do not leave the water filter in when *your* sheet says to pull it (Pro; ANZ Express).
- Do not run Express, Pro, Touch, Bambino, and Plus as one combo.
- Do not treat flashing CLEAN/DESCALE (Express) or 1 CUP+2 CUP (Plus) as descale — those are tablet flushes.
- Do not put CLR, Iron Out, or bleach in the tank. Do not mix acid with bleach.
- Do not copy Instant Pot vinegar on the inner pot, or a Keurig 12-count, onto a Breville group head.

## If scale comes back

Hard water keeps depositing calcium carbonate. [Test the tap](/articles/how-to-test-water-hardness-at-home). Recurring scale is hardness, not a failed sachet. Express: replace the tank filter every two months, sooner in hard water. Touch: three months or 10.6 USgal / 40 L at hardness 4. This page does not size a softener. Distilled is not the everyday fill — Breville says no.

## FAQ

**Can I use vinegar?**
Not on this page. Current BES870 / 878 / 880 / 450 / 500 books name powder or agent, not vinegar. Sage: vinegar may harm rubber seals and gaskets. Urnex: harder to rinse, lingering taste. No vinegar workaround.

**Citric powder from the grocery aisle?**
Dezcal’s ingredient list is already citric (plus sulfamic). Food-grade citric is the same acid family. We are not linking a grocery jar and we are not printing a pantry recipe.

**Filter in or out?**
Out when the sheet says so. Pro (and ANZ Express): remove before powder, back after rinse. US Express V23 descale block does not reprint that NOTE. Bambino / Plus booklets we fetched do not list a tank filter. Touch: follow the screen.

**Bambino vs Express?**
Different heaters (ThermoJet vs thermocoil), different alerts, different buttons. Bambino is 2 Cup + Steam for 5 seconds. Express is 2-CUP then POWER, then 1-CUP and the steam dial. Do not swap them.

**Light still on?**
Finish the rinse pass *your* book prints, then the quit/READY step. Or you cancelled the Bambino/Plus alert without running the cycle. Repeat the full descale for that model. Do not invent a reset combo.

**Can I use CLR?**
No. Tank is water plus labeled descaler. CLR vs Iron Out is a different surface job.
