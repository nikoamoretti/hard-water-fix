## Fingernail test after a wash

Park in shade. Panel cool. Wash the car first so you are looking at mineral, not road film. Dry it. Then do this on a patch in daylight.

1. Run a fingernail across the ring.
2. Wet that same patch. Look again.

CarCareTruth: three tiers. Surface mineral is a chalky ring of calcium carbonate sitting on intact clear coat. It wipes partly off with a wet microfiber. It lifts with a mild acid in **30 to 60 seconds**. Shallow etch survives the acid wipe but you cannot catch it with a fingernail. Visible under angled light. Deep crater: the fingernail catches the rim. Visible halo. That is a body shop, not a longer soak.

Light rings that feel smooth and vanish while wet are film. Heavier spots feel slightly rough. If the ring is identical wet and dry, and a fingernail catches, you are already into the clear coat. Household acid will not un-etch paint.

CarCareTruth: misidentifying surface mineral as etch grinds off clear coat the chemistry would have left intact. Misidentifying a deep crater as a polish job can take you into basecoat. Wet microfiber first. Mild acid second. Fingernail third.

## 50/50 vinegar on a cool shaded panel

Faint white rings you can barely feel. Distilled white vinegar is about 5% acetic acid. CarCareTruth: a 1-to-1 vinegar-to-water mix on a fresh Type 1 spot, **30-to-60-second** dwell on a cool panel, dissolves most fresh deposits in one pass. The same dilution lacks punch for aged or baked deposits.

Cool panel only. Hot clear coat dries the mix into a new ring. CarCareTruth: vinegar straight on a hot panel can hydrolyze the same melamine crosslinks acid rain attacks. Shade. Short dwell. Thorough rinse.

4. Spray the *paint* only. Or wet a microfiber and park it on the spots so runoff does not sit in the sun.
5. Sit **30-60 seconds**. Re-spray if it starts to dry. Do not leave it in the sun.
6. Wipe with microfiber. Rinse with [distilled water](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07VD4KN28?tag=hardwaterfi04-20). Buff dry.
7. Wet the patch again. Judge it wet. Then fingernail it.

If two wet passes lift the rings and the fingernail does not catch, stop. You had film. Skip polish.

If the ring is still there after **two failed acid passes**, vinegar is done. That is etch, not a longer soak. Stop chemistry.
