## Hard-water signs on a shower grab bar

Know the stain before you open the vinegar.

- Faint white haze on the bar after splash dries: early mineral film. Short 50/50 wipe and a dry-after-shower habit stretch the gap.
- Raised white chalk a fingernail catches on the face or flange ring: carbonate scale. Soften 50/50, 10–15 minutes max. Soft cloth. Rinse. Dry. Do not soak into the wall.
- Streaks that return the same day: you left the bar wet. Dry fully after the wipe and after every shower.
- Orange freckles at a scratch or screw: rust, not this calcium path alone. Iron Out on metal freckles only. Never with CLR. Replace a rusted-through or loose safety bar.
- Powder coat or warm-touch wrap that dulls after a harsh acid: stop. Dilute vinegar wipe only if the maker allows. No CLR on those finishes.
- Loose flange, spinning screw, or bar that moves under load: stop chemistry. Fix the mount or call a pro. Safety hardware first.
