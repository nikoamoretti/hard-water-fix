## Prevention

Prevention is a dry face after the next shower, not a harsher acid.

- After each shower, wipe the **strip faces** dry with a towel or a dedicated cloth. Wet faces chalk overnight and hold poorly the next morning.
- Soft-wipe soap film weekly with mild dish soap before it hardens into scum-plus-chalk.
- Stick to pantry vinegar **50/50** on the faces for routine chalk — soap first, soft cloth or soft brush, rinse, dry fully. No jacket soak. No second acid as a habit.
