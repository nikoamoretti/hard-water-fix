## Grease vs mineral honesty: soap first for grease or oil film

Do the grease check before you open the vinegar. Look at the stain in daylight. Rub a small patch with warm water and a drop of mild dish soap.

- White crust that feels chalky and does not smear brown or oily is **mineral film**. Distilled white vinegar **1:1 spray / dwell** is the job. Soft microfiber. Distilled pantry wipe-dry.
- Brown frying film, yellow cooking-spray haze, or a slick hood ring that soap lifts is **oil film**, not calcium. Honesty: **grease or oil film ≠ white mineral.** Soap first. Rinse. Then, if white chalk is still on the glass, do the vinegar mineral pass.
- Both can live on the same splashback. Vinegar will not degrease frying oil. Soap will not dissolve chalk. Treat them in that order: **soap first**, vinegar second.

A mineral spray on top of thick grease wastes the acid and leaves pickle smell in the oil. Wipe soap, rinse, then spray.
