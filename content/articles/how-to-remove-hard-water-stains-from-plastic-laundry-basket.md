## Hard-water signs on a plastic laundry basket

Know the film before you open the vinegar.

- Lint, hair, or gray detergent dust that dish soap lifts: leftover laundry film. **Soap first.** Then judge the chalk on the vents.
- Faint white haze on the vents or lattice after wet clothes drip and dry: early mineral film. Distilled white vinegar **soak or spray**, **soft nylon brush** the **vents**, **distilled pantry rinse**, **dry**.
- Raised white chalk a fingernail catches in the vents, on the lattice crossings, or on the rim: carbonate scale. Same soak or spray. Soft nylon brush the vents. Distilled pantry rinse. Dry.
- Cloudy “dirty hamper” look that soap alone does not clear: often mineral, not dirt. Honesty: chalk reads as dirty until vinegar softens it. Lint is the other film — soap first.
