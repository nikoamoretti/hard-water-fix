## Vinegar 1:1 boil-and-soak. Soft bottle brush. Wooden toothpick for the whistle.

1. Confirm the kettle is **stovetop whistling**, not electric. Confirm the metal: stainless or enamel for this pass; skip strong acid on **aluminum**.
2. Empty old water. Rinse loose flakes. Do not start a mineral boil on top of tea leaves or heavy sludge.
3. Mix **1:1** distilled white vinegar (5% pantry bottle) and water. Fill the kettle enough to cover the chalked floor and reach into the spout throat — do not overfill so boiling mix floods the burner.
4. Set on the stovetop. Bring to a **gentle boil**. When it reaches a boil (or the whistle starts), kill the heat. Let it **soak** — stay in the room. Typical clock: sit **15–30 minutes** after the boil so the acid works the chalk. Do not leave a roaring boil unattended.
5. After the soak, dump the vinegar mix carefully. Soft sponge on the interior floor. Soft **bottle brush** into the spout throat — soft nylon only. No steel wool. No green pad that scars enamel.
6. Clear the **whistle channel**. Remove the spout cap / whistle if it lifts off. Use a **wooden toothpick** (or soft brush tip) to clear chalk from the whistle orifice after the soak. **Do not drill** the orifice. Do not force a metal pick that widens or cracks the hole.
7. Rinse thoroughly with clean water until the kettle **does not smell like pickle**. Food-contact path — rinse until no vinegar smell remains. Optional pantry **distilled water** rinse so tap spots do not flash-dry back onto the floor — grocery jug, pantry text. No Amazon distilled URL (NONE_QUALIFY).
8. **One plain-water boil after.** Fill with fresh water (pantry distilled when you can), bring to a boil, dump. That clears leftover vinegar taste before the next tea fill.
9. Air-dry with the lid / spout open when you can. Refill only when the interior is dry if you are parking the kettle.

One 1:1 boil-and-soak clears most fresh chalk and often restores a muted whistle once the channel is clear. Raised scale that survives one pass may need a second boil-and-soak and another soft brush / toothpick pass — still no drill, still no CLR. Vinegar will not rebuild a cracked whistle cap or a ruined aluminum body — replace damaged parts when the metal or whistle is mechanically failed. Do not invent a replacement ASIN on this page.

Never mix leftover vinegar with bleach. Poison Control: bleach plus an acid, including vinegar, forms chlorine gas. Leave the area. Fresh air. Poison Help **1-800-222-1222**. If bleach was ever used in the kettle, rinse with plain water until it does not smell like bleach before any vinegar boil.
