## What that film is

USGS: hardness is dissolved calcium and magnesium. Heat them and you get calcium carbonate — scale — on heaters. USGS names the home coffee maker.

Keurig’s descale hub: scale is non-toxic; left alone it hinders performance. Descale every **3 to 6 months**, or when the DESCALE light comes on (select brewers). The light is a reminder to run *their* cycle, not a hardness sensor. Hard-water houses hit it sooner. [Test the tap](/articles/how-to-test-water-hardness-at-home) if you want the number. Do not size a softener from this page.

## Prep

Empty the reservoir. K-Elite, K-Café, and K-Duo: if a Keurig Water Filter is in the tank, **remove it and set it aside**. K-Supreme Smart: same, if applicable. No pod. Duo: no ground coffee in the basket either. Large ceramic mug, not paper. Sink nearby. Disable Auto Off if *your* sheet says to. Reservoir sizes and buttons are not one combo.

## Official bottle how-to (Classic, Café, Elite)

This is the queued official bottle because current Keurig manuals name it, not a bake-off vs vinegar. K-Café and K-Elite US Use & Care Guides, and Keurig’s Traditional grouping (K-Classic, K-Café, K-Latte, K-Elite, K-Compact, K-Select), print the same mix and **at least 12** largest-size rinses. About **45 minutes**. The start button is *not* the same.

You need [Keurig Descaling Solution](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00FOT9W5Q?tag=hardwaterfi04-20), fresh water, a large ceramic mug, and a sink.

1. Empty the reservoir. Filter out if *your* sheet names one. No pod.
2. Power the brewer **off**.
3. Pour the **entire bottle** of [Keurig Descaling Solution](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00FOT9W5Q?tag=hardwaterfi04-20) into the empty reservoir. Fill that empty bottle with water and pour that in too. Power back **on**.
4. Mug on the drip tray. Rinsing brew, **largest size**, no pod. Dump in the sink. Classic and Café: lift and lower the handle, then the largest size (Café also presses brew). Elite: **Hot Water**, then the largest size. Do not copy a Supreme hold onto these machines.
5. Repeat until **ADD WATER** lights. No pod.
6. Leave the brewer **on** for at least **30 minutes**. Do not brew. This is the sit the sheets print.
7. Discard leftover solution. Rinse the reservoir thoroughly.
8. Fresh water to the **MAX** line, not past it. Largest-size rinsing brews, no pod, dump each one. Complete **at least 12**. You may refill the tank at least once. Do not cut this short because there is no vinegar smell. Same rinse count as Keurig’s bottle instructions.

Keurig: foam in the mug is the solution hitting scale. Heavily scaled machines may spit air and almost nothing. Then power off, unplug, dump leftover solution, rinse the reservoir, refill with water, plug in, and keep doing Step 8. If that still stalls, sit unplugged at least 30 minutes, then continue the rinses.

Put the charcoal filter back after the rinses.

## Supreme and Duo are different clocks

Do not paste the Classic 12 onto these.

**K-Supreme (button model).** US Use & Care: about **15–20 minutes**. Entire bottle, then a bottle of water. Plugged in and powered **off**, hold **8 oz and 12 oz together for 3 seconds**. When flashing, press **brew**. Repeat until **ADD WATER**. Empty, rinse, fill to MAX. Keep brewing when it flashes until **DESCALE** turns off. That sheet does not print a 30-minute sit or a count of 12.

**K-Supreme Smart.** The screen asks **14 oz bottle** or **2 oz pouch**. This page is the bottle. Remove the water filter if applicable. Follow prompts until **DESCALE COMPLETE**.

**K-Duo (Gen 1).** About **75 minutes**. Pull the filter. Entire bottle, then a bottle of water. **POD** + **12**, brew, dump. Carafe on the plate: **CARAFE** + **12**, brew, dump; power off the heating plate. Sit **30 minutes**. Then four **12-cup** carafe rinses and four **12 oz** pod rinses.

**K-Duo Essentials Gen 2.** Hold **CUPS and OZ together for 3 seconds**. Flashing brew: single-serve descale, then carafe (Keurig: that carafe pass is only a **partial** brew). Rinse: two single-serve, then one carafe that empties the tank. **ADD WATER** on; it exits Descale Mode and the light turns off.

**Mini / K-Mini.** Smaller tank. That booklet uses **one-third** of the bottle (**4.6 oz**) plus water to the fill line, a 30-minute sit, then three fresh-water rinses — not twelve. Do not pour a Classic reservoir mix into a Mini.

## If DESCALE stays on

Keurig: the notification turns off when you **finish the full descaling procedure**. K-Express prints it in one line. K-Supreme: rinse until DESCALE turns off. Duo Essentials Gen 2: the light turns off when it exits Descale Mode after those rinses. Older Classic-family notes: if the red light is still on after the procedure, **repeat the descaling procedure**.

If it stays on, the rinses were skipped, the bottle mix never ran, or a vinegar tank ran on a machine waiting for *this* cycle. Cite Keurig. Do not invent a firmware combo. Café and Elite: still a partial cup after you have repeated the descale **two times** — call Keurig.

## What not to do

- Do not mix descaling solution with vinegar in the same tank, and do not run a vinegar cycle on this page.
- Do not skip the fresh-water rinses because the bottle does not smell like salad. Classic / Café / Elite still want **at least 12**.
- Do not leave the charcoal filter in if *your* sheet says to pull it. Do not descale with a pod in the holder.
- Do not copy Classic 12, Supreme Descale Mode, and Duo carafe rinses into one invented combo.
- Do not pour CLR, Lime-A-Way, Bar Keepers Friend, or dishwasher rinse aid into a brew path. Do not mix any acid with bleach.

Not a [Ninja](/articles/how-to-descale-ninja-coffee-maker). Not an Instant Pot. Not a [kettle](/articles/how-to-descale-an-electric-kettle). Those clocks are different machines.

## If scale comes back

Hard water keeps depositing calcium carbonate. [Test the tap](/articles/how-to-test-water-hardness-at-home). Recurring scale is hardness, not a failed bottle. Replace the charcoal cartridge on *its* schedule (Duo Essentials names about every 2 months). This page does not size a whole-house softener.

K-Elite, K-Café, K-Supreme, and K-Duo: bottled or filtered water if tap taste is off. **Do not use distilled water.** That is Keurig, not a folk tip.

If a vinegar run left pickle coffee or a stuck light, run the official [Keurig Descaling Solution](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00FOT9W5Q?tag=hardwaterfi04-20) cycle above — including the full rinse count — not a second vinegar bath.

## FAQ

**Can I use vinegar anyway?**
Yes on the other URL. Current manuals prefer the bottle. Older keurig.com PDFs still list white vinegar as Method 2. In-warranty, or a DESCALE light that will not die: their bottle. Vinegar steps are not copied here.

**Can I use citric powder?**
The bottle’s SDS is already citric. Grocery food-grade citric is the same acid family as the citric-vs-vinegar page. This page does not print a powder recipe or a buy link. Do not invent a Keurig powder.

**Will lemon juice work?**
Lemon is a weaker citric stand-in, not Keurig’s cycle. No ounces invented here. Use the bottle, or the vinegar URL.

**Classic vs Supreme vs Café vs Duo?**
Classic / Café / Elite: entire bottle + bottle of water, 30-minute sit, **at least 12** largest rinses. Elite starts the solution pass on Hot Water. Supreme: Descale Mode (8 oz + 12 oz, 3 seconds), rinse until DESCALE is off; no 12 on that sheet. Duo: pod *and* carafe. Mini: one-third bottle. Open the booklet that matches the machine. Reservoir sizes differ. Do not say all Keurigs.

**Do I leave the water filter in?**
No, if *your* sheet names one. Elite, Café, Duo, and Supreme Smart: remove the Keurig Water Filter before descaling, set it aside, put it back after the fresh-water rinses.

**The DESCALE light is still on.**
Keurig: finish the full procedure. Rinses skipped, or the bottle never ran, are the usual causes. Repeat the bottle cycle. Do not invent a reset combo.
