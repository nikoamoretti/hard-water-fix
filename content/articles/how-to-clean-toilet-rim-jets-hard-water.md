## What the swirl is (rim jets + siphon jet)

1-Tom-Plumber: toilet rim jets are tiny holes under the rim of the bowl. When you flush, water from the tank comes through those holes and makes the swirl. That swirl is flush power, and it rinses the china. HomeTips: mineral deposits (limescale) clog those openings. Less water enters the bowl. The flush looks weak or one-sided.

The siphon jet is not the same hole. 1-Tom-Plumber: it is one larger hole at the bottom of the bowl. It sends a rush of water that helps pull the swirl down the drain. HomeFixer101 and The Plumbing Directory both name that front hole too. Rim jets wash. The siphon jet starts the siphon. Calcium in either one steals the flush.

Kohler Assist: poor flush or poor rim wash may be caused by obstructed rim holes. Hard water deposits restrict flow through those holes. Kohler’s close-coupled homeowners guide: over time, hard water deposits may clog toilet rim holes and trapways. Use a commercial cleaner recommended for hard water deposits, and follow that cleaner’s instructions.

White or crusty buildup in the holes is calcium. HomeTips: a small mirror under the rim shows it. Orange in the jets is iron, not this mineral. That job is [how to remove rust stains from a toilet](/articles/how-to-remove-rust-stains-from-toilet-hard-water). Do not merge the two.

## Shut off. Flush. Vinegar down the overflow tube.

The jets do not drink from the puddle in the bowl. HomeFixer101: vinegar poured into the bowl never touches the rim jets. The rim channel is fed from the tank. HomeTips and HomeFixer101: the overflow tube — the tall, narrow plastic tube standing in the tank — is the path into that channel.

Distilled white vinegar. Grocery bottle. Pantry. No Amazon link.

1. Find the shutoff, usually on the wall or floor behind the toilet. HomeTips: close it (turn clockwise). Do not crank past the stop.
2. Flush. HomeTips: this empties most of the water from the tank. 1-Tom-Plumber: flush the tank to empty it. The tank should not refill.
3. Lift the tank lid. Set it on a towel. HomeFixer101: look for that vertical overflow tube.
4. Put a funnel in the top of the overflow tube. HomeTips: slowly pour about **2 to 4 cups** of white vinegar so it flows into the internal rinse passages. HomeFixer101 uses about a gallon of distilled white vinegar for a full rim fill. Start with HomeTips’ 2 to 4 cups. Add more only if the tube will take it without backing into the tank. The point is the rim channel, not a tank dump.
5. Timed sit. HomeTips: at least **2 to 3 hours**. Heavier buildup: HomeTips allows overnight. 1-Tom-Plumber: wait about **8 hours**. Kohler Assist, for a hard-water or lime-dissolving cleaner sitting in the rim chamber: **8 to 24 hours**. Vinegar can sit that long in the rim. CLR cannot. That clock is the next section.
6. Open the shutoff. Let the tank refill. HomeTips: flush several times to rinse the passages and clear loosened deposits. HomeFixer101: 3–4 flushes in a row.

Gloves. Open a window or run the fan. USGS: vinegar is an acid; the acidity helps dissolve mineral so water can carry it away. 1-Tom-Plumber: vinegar that ran out of the jets is still acid — wash it off your hands.

If someone already used bleach in this bowl or tank, flush and rinse with plain water before any vinegar. Never mix acid and bleach. Poison Control: mixing bleach with an acid, including vinegar, forms chlorine gas. Washington State DOH names vinegar as one of those acids, and rust-removal products too. Leave the area and breathe fresh air if you already mixed them. Poison Help **1-800-222-1222**.

1-Tom-Plumber also pours a gallon down the flush-valve outlet after lifting the flapper, and Kohler Assist says to lift the flapper or canister and pour a lime-dissolving cleaner into the rim inlet so it sits in the rim chamber. Same idea: fill the rim, not the bowl. This how-to stays on the overflow-tube funnel.

## Mirror, then a nylon zip-tie or wooden toothpick

After the soak, look. HomeTips: a small mirror up under the rim. White or crusty rings around the holes are scale. HomeFixer101: selfie camera works if that is what you have.

HomeTips: gently insert a zip tie or small nylon brush into each rim-jet opening to loosen buildup. Wooden toothpick for the same job. Slow in-and-out. Do not ream the hole larger. Do the siphon jet at the front of the bowl the same way — larger hole, same calcium.

**Not a metal hanger as the plan.** 1-Tom-Plumber names a cut wire coat hanger if vinegar did not finish the job. Kohler Assist names a paper clip or finishing nail. Skip metal as the tool. A hanger can scratch the glaze. It can also break off in the hole. The Plumbing Directory: don’t use anything that could break off inside. HomeFixer101: porcelain scratches, and a scratch is a rough spot where scale returns faster. Nylon zip-tie or wooden toothpick. Mirror first.

Not pumice in a jet. Pumice is the bowl-ring path on [how to remove hard water stains from a toilet bowl](/articles/how-to-remove-hard-water-stains-from-toilet-bowl). A stick does not belong in a rim hole.

Flush again. Watch the swirl. HomeTips: cleaning the jets often restores a strong flush when the toilet flushes weakly, swirls unevenly, or sends too little water around the rim. It does not usually change the standing water level in the bowl.

## FAQ

**Is a weak flush a bad flapper or clogged jets?**

Look under the rim. HomeTips: crust in the holes is mineral. Cleaning the jets restores swirl; it does not usually change the standing water level. TOTO: a worn flapper, a chain with the wrong slack, or a tank waterline set off the overflow tube also makes a weak flush. 1-Tom-Plumber: dump a 5-gallon bucket into the bowl — start slow, then fast. If that siphon is still slow, the problem is not the rim jets. HomeFixer101: if two descales do not bring the swirl back, check the flapper next.

No. The ring sits at the waterline. The jets sit under the rim, plus the siphon jet at the front of the bowl. Bowl ring: [how to remove hard water stains from a toilet bowl](/articles/how-to-remove-hard-water-stains-from-toilet-bowl). This page does not recopy it.
