## Hard-water signs on an epoxy countertop

Know the film before you open the vinegar.

- Faint white haze after the deck dries: early mineral film. Short 50/50 cloth and a dry habit after each rinse stretch the gap.
- Raised chalk a fingernail catches at the faucet flange or sink rim: carbonate scale on the gloss. Soften 1–2 minutes. Soft wipe. Rinse. Dry.
- Cloudy spots that return overnight after a successful clean: rinse water flash-dried — dry habit failed, not “vinegar failed.”
- Smooth dull ring after the crust is gone: scratch or dulled gloss — not leftover calcium. Cleaning removes film; it does not restore factory shine.
- Brown or rusty tint at a metal faucet base: can be iron or metal under splash, not only calcium. Stay on short dilute vinegar first. Do not jump to bathroom lime gel on epoxy.
