Hard-water stains are not a mystery film. They are calcium and magnesium left behind when shower water dries on glass (USGS). Push the water off before it dries and there is nothing to stain. That is a [squeegee](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000CCDBRK?tag=hardwaterfi04-20) after the last shower of the day, about thirty seconds, then a weekly vinegar wipe. A [Rain-X Shower Door](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D6X4PSZ3?tag=hardwaterfi04-20) coating is backup, not a substitute. The brand’s own page says it keeps glass looking clean **for weeks**. It does not say years.

If the door is already chalky, stop here and clean it first. Prevention on top of scale just seals in haze. The removal how-to is the [companion piece on cleaning glass shower doors](/articles/how-to-remove-hard-water-stains-from-glass-shower-doors).

## The 30-second habit

Carolyn Forté at the Good Housekeeping Institute Home Care & Cleaning Lab: squeegee, and dry the shower walls, after the last shower of the day. GH also says a daily squeegee is what keeps grime from stacking up between deep cleans. Same physics as wiping a faucet dry: wet minerals that never sit never become spots.

Working pass:

1. Hang [a squeegee](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000CCDBRK?tag=hardwaterfi04-20) in the stall. If it lives under the sink, you will not use it.
2. After the last shower, start at the top of the door. Overlapping strokes, top to bottom. One blade is enough. The [OXO Good Grips All-Purpose Squeegee](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000CCDBRK?tag=hardwaterfi04-20) is the one we already point to on the stain-removal page: one blade, no ritual.
3. Hit the handle, the hinges, and the **bottom track**. That is where water pools and reseeds the pane.
4. Wipe the puddle the squeegee leaves in the track with a dry cloth. Skip this and you just moved the minerals.

Thirty to forty seconds. Every person, every last shower. Skip a week of this in hard water and you are back on CLR.

Do not razor the glass. Tempered shower doors scratch. The squeegee is a blade for water, not for crust.

## Weekly vinegar, not a second daily chore

GH’s weekly door clean: equal parts distilled white vinegar and water in a spray bottle, **1 teaspoon of dishwashing liquid**, spritz, sit **15 minutes**, wipe with a microfiber, rinse, buff dry. That mix cuts light mineral film and soap scum. It is maintenance, not a descale.

If a fingernail catches the stain, this spray lost. Go to the [removal article](/articles/how-to-remove-hard-water-stains-from-glass-shower-doors) (vinegar for film, CLR for crust, 2-minute clock). Then come back to the squeegee.

Do not let the vinegar mist sit on marble, limestone, travertine, or granite. Those stones take acid badly. Spray the glass. Wipe the stone if you overshoot.

Factory-coated or “easy-clean” glass: skip the acid. Mild detergent and the squeegee. Vinegar and CLR can strip a factory protectant, which is how a coated door goes cloudy while you think you are helping.

## What not to do

- **Do not skip the squeegee because you coated the glass.** Coatings wear. Water still dries.
- **Do not coat a dirty door.** You will lock in film.
- **Do not promise yourself a year of protection.** Rain-X says weeks.
- **Do not use automotive Rain-X, wax, or oil soap as a “hack.”** Wrong chemistry, wrong residue.
- **Do not put vinegar, CLR, or BKF on stone surrounds, or on coated / antique glass.**
- **Do not mix anything on this page with bleach.**
- **Do not treat etching as a stain you failed to prevent.** If the door stays hazy while wet, that is damage. Stop buying bottles.

## FAQ

**Is a shower filter enough to stop stains on the door?**
No. Typical inline shower filters are not softeners. They do not pull calcium and magnesium out of the water. The squeegee still matters. That is a separate article.

**Can I squeegee every other day?**
You can. In hard water you will clean more. The GH habit is after the last shower of the day.

**Will Rain-X make the floor slippery?**
Keep it on the glass. Wipe drips off the pan and the stone. The product is labeled for glass surfaces only.
