## Hard-water signs in a glass teapot

Know the stain before you open the vinegar.

- Faint white haze on the body after tap rinses dry: early mineral film. Vinegar **1:1** body soak, soft bottle brush for the neck, rinse until no taste, dry.
- Raised white chalk a fingernail catches near the waterline, the floor, or the neck: carbonate scale. Same **1:1** soak. Soft brush. Full taste rinse. Dry.
- Brown tea ring that stays brown after a mineral soak: **tannin**, not calcium. Honesty: **brown tea tannin ≠ white mineral**. Baking soda or soap for tannin. Vinegar for chalk.
- Cloudy glass that stays cloudy after chalk is gone: etched or scratched surface. Vinegar clears mineral; it does not polish glass clear again.
- Sour pickle taste in the next pot: leftover vinegar film in the neck or under the lid. Rinse again until there is **no vinegar taste**. Dry.
