## Raised crust. Plastic scraper. Baking-soda paste mechanical only.

Raised white crust a fingernail catches near a faucet base, along a shower curb, or in a low floor spot. That is carbonate scale on the sealer, not a dye in the chips.

1. Soften with [Miracle Sealants Daily](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DJWS5TDF?tag=hardwaterfi04-20). Soft wipe or soft brush.
2. Plastic scraper or plastic putty knife only — hold nearly flat. Lift crust. No metal blade. No razor on the chips or sealer.
3. Mix baking soda with a little water to a thick paste. Peanut-butter thick. Mechanical only. Spread on leftover chalk. Sit a few minutes. Do not let it dry hard.
4. Soft wipe. Rinse with clean water. Buff completely dry.
5. Do not “boost” the paste with vinegar. Acid etches marble chips. Acid plus bicarbonate is salt, water, and CO2 — the fizz is gas, not extra cleaning. That mix myth already lives on [baking soda vs vinegar](/articles/baking-soda-vs-vinegar-hard-water-stains).

If the crust is gone and a smooth dull ring remains — same wet and dry — that can be etch on the marble chips or worn sealer. Cleaning will not polish it back. Honesty first.

## Hard-water signs on terrazzo

Know the film before you open a bottle.

- Faint white haze after the floor or wall dries: early mineral film. pH-neutral spray and a dry habit after each rinse stretch the gap between deep cleans.
- Raised chalk a fingernail catches at a faucet flange or shower curb: carbonate scale. Soften, plastic scrape, baking-soda paste mechanical.
- Water drop no longer beads on a dry patch: tired or missing sealer. Clean the crust. Reseal with a terrazzo- or stone-labeled product when the maker allows. This page does not sell the sealer.
- Smooth dull ring after the crust is gone: etch on marble chips or worn sealer — not leftover scale. More cleaner will not restore gloss.

## Reseal when water stops beading

After the crust is gone and the surface is dry, repeat the water-drop check. If a tablespoon still beads, the sealer is working — keep the pH-neutral daily and wipe dry after splash.

If the drop darkens the matrix, the impregnator or topical sealer is tired. Reseal with a product labeled for terrazzo or natural stone. Follow the maker’s cure time before you wet the floor again. A dull ring that stays after cleaning can be etch on marble chips or worn sealer — refinishing is a different job than mineral removal.
