Chrome will take a short acid pass. It will not take an overnight vinegar sock, a dry sprinkle of cleanser, or a lime remover left to crust on the base. Hard-water spots are calcium and magnesium dried in the fingerprints. Take them off on a timer, rinse, **wipe dry**. The dry is the whole maintenance plan.

If the faucet is not actually chrome, stop and use the finish table. Matte black and oil-rubbed bronze do not get this article’s bottles.

## Chrome method (short contact)

Start with dish soap and a microfiber. Rinse. Dry. If white rings remain, step up.

**[Bar Keepers Friend Soft Cleanser]** is the bottle for chrome.

1. Spot-test the underside of the spout.
2. Shake. Wet the faucet. Squeeze a little on a damp sponge.
3. Work a thin coat. **Rinse thoroughly within one minute.** Do not let it dry on the chrome.
4. Wipe dry with a second cloth.
5. Repeat a fresh one-minute pass on the crusty ring at the base if needed.

Soft Cleanser, not the powder. No green scouring pad, no Magic Eraser.

## CLR only for crust you can feel

If BKF leaves a ridge of scale where the spout meets the sink, that is [CLR] territory — 50/50, **two minutes**, cloth on the crust, not a pour down the body. Towel off stone. Rinse with cold water. Dry. Do not wrap a CLR-soaked rag and leave the room.

## What not to do

- **Do not wrap the faucet in a vinegar sock overnight.**
- Do not use CLR or BKF on matte black or oil-rubbed bronze.
- Do not mix either with bleach.
- Do not use steel wool or a green pad.
- Do not skip the dry.

## Keep it off

Wipe the faucet after the last use of the day. If the white ring returns every morning, clean the aerator.

## FAQ

**Will Bar Keepers Friend scratch chrome?**
Used wet, one minute, rinsed, on actual chrome: BKF lists faucets as a use. A dry powder rub or a green pad will scratch.

**Can I use vinegar instead?**
Yes on chrome, 50/50, short contact, rinse, dry. It is slower than BKF on a crusty base.

**The base is still white after two timed passes.**
Clean or replace the aerator. Then one more 2-minute CLR on the ring. If the chrome is already pitted, stop.
