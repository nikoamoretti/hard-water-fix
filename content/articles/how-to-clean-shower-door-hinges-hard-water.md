## The pivot. Not the channel. Not the pane.

REXI: soap scum, hard water deposits, and dirt pack the hinge. That buildup makes the door stiff. In some cases the hinge sticks.

- **Pivot / knuckle** — glass-to-glass or wall-mount hinge on a frameless or hinged door. The pin sits in that joint. Mineral packs the moving face.
- **Chrome or stainless** — the only finishes that get CLR. REXI’s mill job is a toothbrush in the crevices, then a rinse and a dry.
- **Brass, oil-rubbed bronze, painted** — mechanical wipe only. Then **stop**. CLR’s stay-off list includes brass and painted, coated, or sealed surfaces. CLR can take the finish off brass and pit it.
- **Threshold** — marble, limestone, granite next to the curb. CLR: avoid natural stone. Keep acid off that stone.

You need distilled white vinegar from the pantry (grocery bottle, no Amazon vinegar). A soft toothbrush. A cloth. ## 1. Vinegar 50/50. Toothbrush in the knuckle.

**On the door.** Distilled white vinegar. Grocery bottle. Pantry. No Amazon link.

REXI: mix equal parts vinegar and water. Spray the hinges. Let it sit **a few minutes** so the vinegar can dissolve hard water deposits. Then a soft-bristled brush or toothbrush in the crevices. Rinse. Dry.

1. Wipe hair and grit off the hinge dry. REXI: start by removing loose dirt from the nooks.
2. Mix **50/50** distilled white vinegar and water. Cloth or spray. Coat the pivot. Not a soak of the whole door in a bucket.
3. Sit **a few minutes**. REXI clock. Not overnight in the knuckle.
4. Soft toothbrush in the joint. Small circles. REXI: pay attention to the joints and the edges. Not a metal pick. Not a grout saw.
5. Rinse. Then dry the crevices. REXI: leaving the hinges wet can rust them.
6. Repeat a timed pass if crust remains. Do not extend the clock into an overnight sit in the pin.

Keep the mix off adjacent stone. Marble and limestone *are* calcium carbonate.

Never mix vinegar and bleach. Poison Control: mixing bleach with an acid, including vinegar, forms chlorine gas. Leave the area and breathe fresh air if you already mixed them. Poison Help **1-800-222-1222**.

## 2. Distilled rinse. Tap water puts the mineral back.

Hard tap water is how the scale got into the knuckle. After vinegar (and after CLR, if you use it), rinse with [Amazon Grocery Distilled Water, 1 gal](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07VD4KN28?tag=hardwaterfi04-20). Then towel the pivot dry.

## 4. Orange rust on the hinge only: Iron Out. Never with CLR.

White or tan crust is scale. Orange or brown on the metal is rust. Those are different jobs. The split lives on [CLR vs Iron Out](/articles/clr-vs-iron-out). Do not mix them. Iron Out’s SDS: contact with acids liberates toxic gas. CLR is the acid. Queue them. Do not cocktail them.

## FAQ

**The glass is still spotted. Did I do this wrong?**

You cleaned the wrong part. The pane is the [glass-door article](/articles/how-to-remove-hard-water-stains-from-glass-shower-doors). The channel is the [track article](/articles/how-to-clean-shower-door-tracks-hard-water).

**Vinegar or CLR in the hinge?**

Light film on chrome or stainless: grocery vinegar, 50/50, a few minutes, toothbrush. Heavy crust on that same chrome or stainless: [CLR](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C4G2BJKX?tag=hardwaterfi04-20), two minutes, then rinse. Brass, oil-rubbed bronze, painted: mechanical, then stop. Comparison: [vinegar vs CLR](/articles/vinegar-vs-clr-for-hard-water-stains).

**Can I leave vinegar in the knuckle overnight?**

No. REXI’s sit is a few minutes. Overnight in the pin is not this how-to.

**The door is still stiff after a clean. Now what?**

A pitted, seized hinge is replacement, not more acid. REXI: corrosion can weaken the hinge until it breaks. Stop the acid. Replace the hardware.

**Can I use Iron Out and CLR together?**

No. White crust is CLR. Orange rust is Iron Out. [CLR vs Iron Out](/articles/clr-vs-iron-out): do not mix them. Iron Out plus acid can liberate toxic gas.

**Can I put Rain-X Shower Door on the hinges?**

No. That product is glass. Keep it off the hardware.

**Can I mix vinegar or CLR with bleach?**

No. Poison Control: bleach plus an acid, including vinegar, forms chlorine gas. Leave the area. Fresh air. **1-800-222-1222**.
