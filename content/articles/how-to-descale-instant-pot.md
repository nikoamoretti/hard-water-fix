There is no Descale button on an Instant Pot. Instant Brands does not run a kettle boil or a coffee-maker CLEAN cycle through the cooker. The white film is on the removable stainless inner pot. US Duo and Duo Plus manuals want a vinegar-dampened sponge on that insert — not acid in the cooker housing.

## What that film is

USGS: hardness is dissolved calcium and magnesium. Heat them and you get calcium carbonate — scale. USGS names the home coffee maker. Same minerals cloud an Instant Pot insert.

The cloud shows on the cooking pot you lift out, usually at the waterline and on the floor. Instant Pot’s US FAQ calls a bluish stain rainbowing: high heat, starchy food, or detergent. Harmless on 304 stainless. White hard-water spots are the mineral film. Instant Pot’s Care & Cleaning page: minerals or salt in food and water cause the discoloration.

It is not on the cooker base. The heating element lives in the housing. Instant Brands: do not put liquid or food of any kind into the cooker base. Do not immerse the appliance. Do not rinse it under a faucet.

Stock inner pots are food-grade 304 (18/8) stainless, no chemical coating, dishwasher-safe (Instant Pot FAQ). Duo, Duo Plus, Duo Mini, Pro, and Duo Crisp sheets we fetched all send hard-water stains to vinegar on that insert. Duo Crisp still wants a damp cloth on the outer housing. If you bought a ceramic-coated aluminum insert (Duo V5 lists it as a replacement part), skip the acid pass unless that accessory sheet allows it. Many Instant Pots ship stainless. Confirm yours.

## Instant Brands how-to (stainless inner pot)

Open the booklet that matches the cooker. Duo, Duo Plus, Mini, and Pro print the same stain line. Do not copy a kettle recipe or invent one combo for every lid.

Always unplug. Let every part cool.

1. Cancel, then unplug. Instant Brands: cool to room temperature before cleaning.
2. Lift out the inner cooking pot. Food and liquid belong in the pot, never the housing.
3. Wash the stainless insert with hot water and mild dish soap, or run it in the dishwasher. Instant Brands allows both. Some discoloration after machine washing will not affect safety or performance.
4. White mineral film: a vinegar-dampened sponge and scrubbing. Distilled white vinegar. US Duo V5 and Duo Plus V4 do not print a cup-to-water mix for this stain. They print the sponge.
5. Instant Pot’s Care & Cleaning page (Instant Pot ME) goes further than the US sponge line: **1 cup of white vinegar** in the bottom of the inner pot, rest **5 minutes**, pour out, rinse. Same insert. Still not the housing. White hard-water stains on that page also take a damp sponge soaked in vinegar or lemon.
6. Rinse. Dry the exterior before the pot goes back in the base.
7. Cooker base and condensation rim: a barely-damp cloth, then air dry. Control panel: a soft, barely-damp cloth or sponge.

That is not a kettle descale. There is no 20-minute boil-and-sit on a hidden element. The heater is in the housing; the acid stays in the removable pot.

## Optional citric (not Instant Brands’ US steps)

Instant Brands’ US Duo and Duo Plus sheets name white vinegar, not citric powder, and not an Instant Pot–branded descaler. Instant Brands does not sell a magic Instant Pot descaler we could find. Do not buy Dezcal-for-IP. That SKU does not exist here.

Food-grade citric acid is the same acid family as many kettle descalers. Instant Pot ME already names lemon for white hard-water stains. Bulk citric is not in the Instant Brands US numbered steps. The mineral job lives on [citric vs vinegar](/articles/citric-acid-vs-vinegar-for-limescale). If you use the powder, keep it in the inner pot, rinse like vinegar, and do not copy a 90 °C UK washer recipe or a kettle boil into this cooker.

Vinegar is grocery. There is no Instant Pot descaler bottle on this page.

## Ring, lid, condensation collector

The silicone sealing ring holds cooking odor. Vinegar can add pickle smell on top of last night’s chili.

US Duo V5 and Duo Plus: store rings in a well-ventilated area. To eliminate odors, add **1 cup (8 oz / 250 mL) water and 1 cup (8 oz / 250 mL) white vinegar** to the inner cooking pot, run **Pressure Cook for 5–10 minutes**, then Quick Steam Release. That cycle is labeled for the ring, not as a descale program. Duo Mini prints the same 1 cup + 1 cup, including on 3-quart. Instant Pot: replace the ring every 12 to 18 months, authorized rings only.

After cleaning, store the lid upside down on the cooker base. Remove the anti-block shield, steam-release valve, and float-valve parts; wash the steam-release pipe so it does not clog. Empty and rinse the condensation collector after each use. Steam rack: top-rack dishwasher or hot soapy water. Never harsh chemical detergents, powders, or scouring pads on accessories.

Do not put bleach in the inner pot. A regional Instant Pot Malaysia FAQ names an overnight bleach soak for the *ring*. US Duo manuals we fetched do not. Acid plus bleach makes chlorine gas (Washington State DOH; vinegar is on their acid list). Skip bleach.

Duo Crisp with Ultimate Lid: Instant Pot FAQ says that sealing ring is permanently attached. If yours does not pull out, do not force it. Check that lid’s sheet.

## What not to do

- Do not pour vinegar, citric, or anything else into the cooker base or onto the heating element.
- Do not immerse the appliance or rinse the housing under a tap.
- Do not copy a kettle descale cycle or a [Keurig](/articles/how-to-descale-a-keurig-with-vinegar) or [Ninja](/articles/how-to-descale-ninja-coffee-maker) CLEAN/descale button. Those clocks are different machines.
- Do not invent an Instant Pot OEM descaler, Instant-branded bottle, or Urnex Dezcal-for-IP.
- Do not put CLR, Lime-A-Way, Bar Keepers Friend, or toilet descaler in the food pot unless Instant Brands names that bottle. The US sheets we fetched do not.
- Do not mix any acid with bleach.
- Do not acid-descale a ceramic or nonstick insert unless that insert’s sheet allows it.
- The stainless inner pot is dishwasher-safe on the US Duo / Duo Plus / FAQ pages. The cooker base is not.

## If the film comes back

Hard water will keep depositing calcium carbonate. [Test the tap](/articles/how-to-test-water-hardness-at-home) if you want the number. Distilled water for cooking slows the film; it is not a whole-house softener, and this page does not size one. Acid comparison: [citric vs vinegar](/articles/citric-acid-vs-vinegar-for-limescale). Coffee-maker bottles vs pantry vinegar: [vinegar vs descaling solution](/articles/vinegar-vs-descaling-solution-coffee-maker). ## FAQ

**Can I use CLR?**
Not from Instant Brands. US Duo and Duo Plus care name vinegar on the inner pot, not CLR. CLR is a surface remover on other pages. Do not put it in a food-path insert unless Instant Brands prints that bottle.

**Can I use citric instead of vinegar?**
Instant Brands’ US steps are vinegar. Instant Pot ME names lemon for white stains. Food-grade citric acid is the same mineral job as the citric-vs-vinegar page, not an Instant Pot OEM descaler, and not in the Instant Brands numbered list.

**Duo vs Mini vs Duo Crisp vs Pro?**
Hard-water line is the same vinegar-dampened sponge on the stainless insert. Mini still prints 1 cup water + 1 cup vinegar for ring odor. Duo Crisp: dishwasher the inner pot; damp cloth on the housing; Ultimate Lid rings may not come off. Pro manuals we fetched match the Duo sponge and odor cycle. Check *your* lid and buttons. Do not mush every model into one invented combo.

**Can I dishwasher the insert?**
Yes on the stainless stock pot. Instant Brands FAQ and the US Duo / Duo Plus manuals say the inner pot is dishwasher-safe. Some discoloration after machine washing does not affect safety or performance. Do not dishwasher the cooker base.

**Is there a descale button?**
No. Instant Brands does not publish a descale mode on Duo / Duo Plus / Mini. Pressure Cook in the odor steps is a cooking program with vinegar in the inner pot, not a hidden kettle cycle.
