## Hard-water signs on an ice scoop

Know the film before you open the vinegar.

- Faint white haze on the bowl or lip after hard-tap melt dries: early mineral film. Vinegar **1:1**, **short soak 5–15 min** (shorter on aluminum), soft sponge, rinse until no smell, dry fully.
- Raised white chalk a fingernail catches on the lip, the underside, or the handle: carbonate scale. Same short soak. Soft sponge. Full rinse. Dry fully.
- Cloudy “dirty ice” look on the scoop that soap alone does not clear: often mineral, not dirt. Honesty: chalk reads as dirty ice until vinegar softens it.
- Aluminum that darkens or looks dull after a long acid sit: that is the metal reacting, not leftover calcium. Honesty: shorten the clock. Do not reach for CLR.
- Scratched plastic that re-hazes fast after every clean: surface harbors film. Prevention (dry fully; distilled pantry rinse) matters more than stronger acid.
