## Hard-water signs in a stainless thermos

Know the stain before you open the vinegar.

- Faint white haze on the walls after tap rinses dry: early mineral film. Short dilute soak, bottle brush, dry the gasket.
- Raised white chalk a fingernail catches near the waterline or neck: carbonate scale. Dilute vinegar **1:3 to 1:1**, short clock. Soft brush. Rinse. Dry gasket.
- Brown tea or coffee ring that stays brown after a mineral soak: **tannin**, not calcium. Soap and a tannin-oriented clean — honesty: vinegar clears chalk first, not every brown stain.
- Sour or pickle smell that returns after rinsing: damp **lid gasket**. Dry the gasket and threads fully; leave the bottle open between uses when you can.
- Exterior water spots on the outer stainless: wipe and dry; this page’s soak is for the **interior** drink path, not a cookware simmer on the outside.
