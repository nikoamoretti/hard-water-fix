## Affresh how-to (mineral path)

Follow the box plus **your** manual. Affresh: all makes and models. Washer SDS is sodium percarbonate and sodium carbonate, no citric. Dishwasher SDS puts citric and sodium bisulfate on this tablet. That citric is labeled chemistry, not a scoop of food-grade powder. Kettles and coffee: [citric acid vs vinegar for limescale](/articles/citric-acid-vs-vinegar-for-limescale).

1. Load as usual, or run empty. Affresh: you can wash a load at the same time. Check dishwasher-safe marks.

2. Placement is the split. With dishes: one tablet of [Affresh dishwasher cleaner](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00SXC85IQ?tag=hardwaterfi04-20) in the **bottom** of the tub; detergent in the dispenser. Empty: one tablet in the **detergent dispenser**, no extra detergent. Heavy buildup: two tablets — one in the dispenser, one on the bottom — empty, after you clear the filter.

3. Run a **normal** wash cycle. Affresh, Whirlpool, and Maytag all name Normal. Do not invent minutes. Add sanitize only if **your** panel names it.

4. Whirlpool: leave the door slightly ajar so the tub can dry.

Affresh: monthly, or about every 30 wash cycles. Extra cycles only if Affresh names heavy soil. Check **your** book.

## What not to do

- Do not treat this as a vinegar-in-the-dishwasher how-to. Maytag: vinegar is an acid and “using it too often could damage your dishwasher.” Whirlpool, Affresh, and GE still list vinegar or citric-crystal DIY. This page follows the labeled cleaners, not a drum scoop of food-grade citric.
- Do not put dishwasher salt in the detergent cup. Salt is the ion-exchange tank, only if you found a cap: [dishwasher salt for hard water](/articles/dishwasher-salt-hard-water).
- Do not drop a **washer** Affresh tablet in the dishwasher. Washer: sodium percarbonate + sodium carbonate. Dishwasher: citric + sodium bisulfate. Washer how-to: [how to clean a washing machine in hard water](/articles/how-to-clean-washing-machine-hard-water).
- Do not treat rinse aid as this cleaner. Rinse aid lives in the door dispenser: [best rinse aid for hard water](/articles/best-rinse-aid-for-hard-water).
- Do not treat Quantum / 3-in-1 detergent tabs as a machine cleaner, and do not treat them as salt.
- Do not mix either cleaner with bleach. Maytag: bleach can damage a stainless tub. Bosch: no bleach-based products to clean the dishwasher.
- Do not run Finish Dual Action with dishes, and do not add detergent to that bottle’s cycle.
- Do not assume every Whirlpool, Bosch, or GE has the same self-clean button.

## If the spots come back

A cleaner cycle treats the machine. It does not change the tap. USGS: calcium and magnesium; heat drops calcium carbonate. [How to test water hardness at home](/articles/how-to-test-water-hardness-at-home) is the Ca+Mg check. This page does not size a whole-house softener.

Cloudy film versus etch: one-minute vinegar **wipe on the glass**, not a vinegar tub cycle. Film lifts. Etch does not. A cleaner will not un-frost etched glass. That split is [cloudy dishwasher glasses](/articles/cloudy-dishwasher-glasses-etching-or-hard-water). Spots on plates: [how to remove hard water spots from dishes](/articles/how-to-remove-hard-water-spots-from-dishes). Sheeting is still rinse aid. A salt cap, if this tub has one, is still salt.

## FAQ

**Which one for hard water spots?**
Affresh is the mineral/limescale tablet. Finish Dual Action also claims limescale, plus grease. Neither is a lab winner for “works better in hard water.” Next-load spots are often rinse aid or hardness.

**Can I skip rinse aid if I run a cleaner?**
No. Cleaner is a maintenance cycle. Rinse aid is a surfactant on every last rinse. Finish still points you at Jet-Dry in the dispenser. How-to: [best rinse aid for hard water](/articles/best-rinse-aid-for-hard-water).

**Empty or full?**
Affresh: either, with different placement (bottom with dishes; dispenser if empty). Finish Dual Action liquid: empty, no detergent. Finish In-Wash tabs are a different SKU.

**Is this the same as washer Affresh?**
No. Washer SDS: sodium percarbonate + sodium carbonate, no citric. Dishwasher SDS: citric acid + sodium bisulfate.

**Can I just use Quantum instead of a cleaner?**
No. Finish US lists Quantum under detergents. Dual Action liquid is the cleaner. All-in-one tabs are not this empty-bottle cycle, and they are not salt.

**Can I mix the tablet and the bottle in one cycle?**
The labels do not say to. Finish Dual Action forbids other soap. Affresh empty uses the detergent cup. Separate cycles, each per its box.
