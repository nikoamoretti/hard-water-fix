## Soap first for crumbs and grease

Do the food-film check before you open the vinegar. Empty the cookies. Look at the stain in daylight. Rub a small patch with warm water and a drop of mild dish soap.

- White crust that feels chalky and does not smear greasy or sugary is **mineral film**. Distilled white vinegar **1:1 soak** is the job. Soft detail brush on the **threads and lid rim**. Distilled pantry rinse and dry. Rinse until **no vinegar smell**.
- Cookie crumbs, butter smear, chocolate film, or a sugary ring that soap lifts is **food film**, not calcium. Honesty: **crumbs and grease ≠ white mineral.** Soap first. Rinse. Then, if white chalk is still on the glass, do the vinegar mineral pass.

A mineral soak on top of crumbs and grease wastes the acid and leaves pickle smell in the fat. Empty, soap, rinse, then soak.

## Etching honesty: permanent etch haze ≠ mineral
