CDC: public systems disinfect with chlorine or chloramine and keep a residual in the pipes. That residual is what hits skin in a hot shower. USGS: hardness is dissolved calcium and magnesium. A fist-sized cartridge has seconds of contact time. Softening needs minutes on resin. The physics does not fit in the box.

Ignore “20-stage.” Stage counts are marketing arithmetic. Judge the **media list**. Ceramic or “energy” beads do not soften anything.

## FAQ

**Will this stop soap scum?**
Soap scum is soap plus hardness minerals. The filter does not take the minerals out. Scum stays.

**Well water?**
If your well is not chlorinated, the chlorine job disappears. Iron and sulfur are a different filter. Hardness on a well is still a softener.

**Does it kill water pressure?**
A fresh inline unit is a small restriction. A dirty one is a bigger one. Replace on the pressure drop, not the calendar you forgot.
